How Each Project Is Built

  1. Understand the problem: who does the work today, and what makes it hard.
  2. Decide the split: what the AI does, what fixed rules do, and when a person takes over.
  3. Build it: rules that must always hold are written in code, not left to the prompt.
  4. Test it: valid requests, unauthorised access, handovers and failures, before it is shown.
