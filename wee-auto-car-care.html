Wee Auto Car Care

By Lee Yih Ven · Published 16 October 2026

Designed for a busy car workshop that sells 13 fixed-price service packages and opens Monday to Saturday. Customers message all day about prices, opening hours, free slots and how their car is doing. Staff working on the cars cannot answer every message at once, and a wrong promise about a price or a slot is hard to take back.

I built a prototype AI customer assistant that answers routine questions, takes booking requests and passes everything else to staff with a set reason. The AI understands each message and writes the reply from the workshop's approved information. Code checks every booking against the package list, the opening hours and a free bay and mechanic before anything is saved.

Staff keep every decision: each booking request waits for staff to confirm or reject it, and bills, repair updates and complaints stay with them. In testing, the assistant passed all 30 customer scenarios, covering valid requests, attempts to see another customer's booking, staff handovers and requests that cannot go through. Replay them, or try it live, on the customer assistant and the staff dashboard.

One Enquiry, From Message to Staff Decision

Follow one customer message to its outcome: a routine answer, a booking request that staff confirm, or a handover to staff.

  1. The customer sends a question or asks for a service. The AI reads the workshop's approved information. It then answers, starts a booking or passes the matter to staff.
  2. A routine question gets an answer from that information, with package prices only. Anything else goes to staff with a set reason and a short summary. Staff call the customer, then mark it done.
  3. For a booking, code checks the service, the opening hours and a free bay and mechanic, and offers other free times if the slot is taken. The customer then gives name, car plate and phone. Code checks again and saves one request per car per day for staff, or explains and saves nothing.
  4. Staff confirm or reject the request on their screen. The AI cannot confirm a booking. Staff phone the customer with the answer.

What It Does for the Workshop

Routine questions get answered, and the rest reach staff

Prices, opening hours and service questions are answered from the workshop's approved information, quoting only the 13 package prices. Bills, repair updates, cancellations, complaints, other prices and requests for a person go to staff with one of eight set reasons and a summary, so staff need not ask again. When a customer's words fit a package but not which one, such as the oil grade, the AI asks a short question instead.

Bookings are checked, then held for staff

A request is saved only for a listed package on an open day within the booking window, with a valid name, car plate and phone, and with a bay and a skilled mechanic free for the whole job. The slot is checked again at the moment of saving, and each car gets one request a day. Every request waits at the top of the staff dashboard until staff confirm or reject it.

Each customer sees only their own booking

A booking is shown only after its booking ID and the last four phone digits match, or to the customer who made that request in the same chat. It shows one booking with its date, time, service and status, never a list, a price or a ready time. Three wrong tries lock the check, and the enquiry goes to staff.

Tools, Resources and Workflow

How Claude works

Claude reads the three resources at the start of each conversation. It calls tools until it can answer, and each result goes back to it. Limits of 600 output tokens and 6 tool rounds stop a reply from looping.

Resource (read)Holds
workshop://policy-guideHours, booking rules, the booking check, the 8 handover reasons.
workshop://faqCommon questions with approved answers.
workshop://service-packages13 packages, the only prices the assistant may quote.
Tool (act)Checked in code
verify_customerBooking ID and phone last 4 must both match, compared as text. Same error whether the booking is missing or the digits are wrong. Locked after 3 failed attempts in one conversation.
get_bookingOnly the booking verified, or requested, in this conversation. One booking, never a list. Date, time, service and status only: no price, ready time or mechanic.
check_availabilityPackage must be in the service package list. No past dates, no Sundays, nothing after the booking window. Bay free and a mechanic with the right skill free for the whole job.
request_bookingPackage must be in the list; name, plate and phone checked. Slot re-checked at the moment of writing. One request per car per day. Saved as pending; booking and customer IDs issued by code.
escalate_to_staffReason must be one of the 8 fixed reasons. Summary required so staff need not re-ask. Saved to the staff queue on the dashboard.

Code Walkthrough

The Booking Check Lives in the Tool

No booking detail is shown until the booking ID and last 4 phone digits match. Three wrong tries lock the check. The lookup returns one booking with safe fields only.

// tools.js: verify_customer and get_booking
      verify_customer({ booking_id, phone_last4 }) {
        if (session.failedVerifications >= MAX_VERIFY_ATTEMPTS)
          return fail("locked", "Booking check locked after 3 failed attempts. Hand the enquiry to staff with reason verification_failed.", false);
        const last4 = String(phone_last4 == null ? "" : phone_last4).trim();
        if (!/^\d{4}$/.test(last4)) return fail("validation", "phone_last4 must be exactly 4 digits.", true);
        const id = normId(booking_id);
        const b = store.load().bookings.find((x) => x.booking_id === id && ACTIVE(x));
        if (!b || digits(b.phone).slice(-4) !== last4) {
          session.failedVerifications += 1;
          const left = MAX_VERIFY_ATTEMPTS - session.failedVerifications;
          return fail("not_verified",
            left > 0 ? `Booking ID and phone digits do not match. ${left} attempt(s) left.`
                     : "Booking ID and phone digits do not match. No attempts left; hand the enquiry to staff with reason verification_failed.",
            left > 0);
        }
        session.verifiedBookingId = id;
        return { ok: true, verified: true, booking_id: id };
      },

      get_booking({ booking_id }) {
        const id = normId(booking_id);
        if (id !== session.verifiedBookingId && !session.ownBookingIds.includes(id))
          return fail("not_verified", "This booking has not been verified in this conversation. Call verify_customer first.", true);
        const b = store.load().bookings.find((x) => x.booking_id === id && ACTIVE(x));
        if (!b) return fail("not_found", "The booking is no longer active. Hand to staff.", false);
        // Safe fields only: no price, no ready time, no mechanic, no other customer.
        return { ok: true, booking: { booking_id: b.booking_id, date: b.date, start_time: b.start_slot, service: b.package_name || b.service, status: b.status } };
      },

Booking Requests Are Saved as Pending

The slot is checked again just before saving. Each car gets one request per day. The status is always pending.

// tools.js: request_booking
      request_booking(input) {
        const pkg = findPackage(input.package_id);
        if (!pkg) return fail("not_in_packages", "Not a package in the service package list. Hand to staff with reason price_not_in_packages_or_inspection.", false);
        const bad = checkDate(input.date);
        if (bad) return bad;
        const name = String(input.customer_name || "").trim();
        const plate = normPlate(input.plate);
        const phone = digits(input.phone);
        if (!name) return fail("validation", "Customer name is missing. Ask the customer.", true);
        if (!/^[A-Z]{1,4}\d{1,4}[A-Z]?$/.test(plate)) return fail("validation", "Plate does not look like a Malaysian registration. Ask the customer to check it.", true);
        if (!/^01\d{8,9}$/.test(phone)) return fail("validation", "Phone must be a Malaysian mobile number starting 01. Ask the customer to check it.", true);

        const state = store.load();
        // Duplicate: same car, same day, already pending or confirmed.
        if (state.bookings.some((b) => b.date === input.date && ACTIVE(b) && normPlate(b.plate) === plate))
          return fail("duplicate", "This car already has a booking on that date. Changes go to staff.", false);
        // Slot re-checked at the moment of writing.
        const slot = String(input.start_slot || "").trim();
        const fit = freeStarts(state, input.date, pkg).find((x) => x.slot === slot);
        if (!fit) return fail("conflict", "That start time is no longer free. Call check_availability again and offer the customer another time.", true);

        // Customer ID: existing customer by phone, otherwise a new one.
        const existing = state.bookings.find((b) => digits(b.phone) === phone && b.customer_id);
        let customerId = existing && existing.customer_id;
        if (!customerId) {
          const maxC = state.bookings.reduce((m, b) => Math.max(m, parseInt(String(b.customer_id || "CUS0").slice(3), 10) || 0), 0);
          customerId = "CUS" + String(maxC + 1).padStart(3, "0");
        }
        const maxB = state.bookings.reduce((m, b) => Math.max(m, parseInt(b.booking_id.slice(3), 10) || 0), 0);
        const bookingId = "SVC" + String(maxB + 1).padStart(3, "0");
        const n = slotsNeeded(pkg.duration_min);
        const i0 = SLOTS.indexOf(slot);
        const svc = SERVICES[pkg.service];
        const pretty = phone.length === 10 ? `${phone.slice(0, 3)}-${phone.slice(3, 6)} ${phone.slice(6)}` : `${phone.slice(0, 3)}-${phone.slice(3, 7)} ${phone.slice(7)}`;
        state.bookings.push({
          booking_id: bookingId, date: input.date, start_slot: slot, slots_occupied: n,
          slot_list: SLOTS.slice(i0, i0 + n), bay: svc.bay, mechanic: fit.mechanics[0],
          service: pkg.service, package_id: pkg.package_id, package_name: pkg.name,
          duration_minutes: pkg.duration_min, ready_at: addMinutes(slot, pkg.duration_min),
          next_bookable_at: SLOTS[i0 + n] || "18:00",
          customer_name: name, plate: input.plate.toUpperCase().trim(), phone: pretty,
          price_rm: pkg.price_rm, status: "pending", customer_id: customerId, source: "assistant",
        });
        store.save(state);
        session.ownBookingIds.push(bookingId);
        return { ok: true, booking_id: bookingId, status: "pending", date: input.date, start_time: slot, package: pkg.name,
                 message: "Booking request saved as pending. Staff will review and confirm it." };
      },

Hand to Staff With a Fixed Reason

The reason must be one of eight. A summary is required.

// tools.js: handover reasons and escalate_to_staff
  const HANDOVER_REASONS = [
    "release_time_or_progress",
    "money",
    "price_not_in_packages_or_inspection",
    "cancel_or_change_booking",
    "complaint",
    "asked_for_person",
    "not_covered",
    "verification_failed",
  ];

      escalate_to_staff(input) {
        if (!HANDOVER_REASONS.includes(input.reason))
          return fail("validation", "reason must be one of: " + HANDOVER_REASONS.join(", "), true);
        const summary = String(input.summary || "").trim();
        if (summary.length < 10) return fail("validation", "Write a summary staff can act on.", true);
        const state = store.load();
        state.handovers = state.handovers || [];
        const id = "HO" + String(state.handovers.length + 1).padStart(3, "0");
        state.handovers.push({
          handover_id: id, reason: input.reason, summary,
          customer_name: input.customer_name || "", contact_phone: input.contact_phone || "",
          booking_id: input.booking_id ? normId(input.booking_id) : "", status: "open",
        });
        store.save(state);
        return { ok: true, handover_id: id, message: "Handed to staff. Tell the customer staff will contact them." };
      },

MCP Server: Resources and Tools

The server lists the resources and tools. Each tool call goes to the shared tools.js.

// mcp_server.mjs
server.setRequestHandler(ListResourcesRequestSchema, async () => ({
  resources: RESOURCE_LIST.map(({ uri, name, mimeType }) => ({ uri, name, mimeType })),
}));

server.setRequestHandler(ReadResourceRequestSchema, async (req) => {
  const r = RESOURCE_LIST.find((x) => x.uri === req.params.uri);
  if (!r) throw new Error(`Unknown resource ${req.params.uri}`);
  return { contents: [{ uri: r.uri, mimeType: r.mimeType, text: resourceText[r.key] }] };
});

server.setRequestHandler(ListToolsRequestSchema, async () => ({
  tools: workshop.tools.map((t) => ({ name: t.name, description: t.description, inputSchema: t.input_schema })),
}));

server.setRequestHandler(CallToolRequestSchema, async (req) => {
  const result = workshop.call(req.params.name, req.params.arguments || {});
  return {
    content: [{ type: "text", text: JSON.stringify(result) }],
    isError: result.ok === false,
  };
});

The Agent Loop

Claude gets each tool result back until it can answer. Errors are flagged. Limits end the loop.

// agent.js: runTurn
    for (let round = 0; round <= limits.maxToolRounds; round++) {
      const res = await fetch("https://api.anthropic.com/v1/messages", {
        method: "POST",
        headers: {
          "content-type": "application/json",
          "x-api-key": args.apiKey,
          "anthropic-version": "2023-06-01",
          "anthropic-dangerous-direct-browser-access": "true",
        },
        body: JSON.stringify({
          model: args.model, max_tokens: limits.maxTokens,
          system: args.system, tools, messages: args.messages,
        }),
      });
      if (!res.ok) {
        const body = await res.text();
        throw new Error(`Anthropic API ${res.status}: ${body.slice(0, 300)}`);
      }
      const data = await res.json();
      Object.keys(usage).forEach((k) => (usage[k] += (data.usage && data.usage[k]) || 0));
      args.messages.push({ role: "assistant", content: data.content });

      const text = data.content.filter((b) => b.type === "text").map((b) => b.text).join("\n").trim();
      if (text) trace.push({ type: "text", text });

      if (data.stop_reason !== "tool_use") {
        return { reply: text, trace, usage, stoppedBy: data.stop_reason };
      }
      const results = [];
      if (round === limits.maxToolRounds) {
        // Close every open tool call so the conversation stays valid, then stop.
        for (const block of data.content.filter((b) => b.type === "tool_use"))
          results.push({ type: "tool_result", tool_use_id: block.id, is_error: true,
            content: JSON.stringify({ ok: false, error: { category: "limit", retryable: false, message: "Tool-round limit reached." } }) });
        args.messages.push({ role: "user", content: results });
        break;
      }
      for (const block of data.content.filter((b) => b.type === "tool_use")) {
        const result = await args.callTool(block.name, block.input);
        trace.push({ type: "tool", name: block.name, input: block.input, result });
        results.push({
          type: "tool_result", tool_use_id: block.id,
          content: JSON.stringify(result), is_error: result && result.ok === false,
        });
      }
      args.messages.push({ role: "user", content: results });
    }

The Rules Claude Follows

What the assistant never does is a fixed list. So is when it hands to staff.

// prompt.js: never and hand to staff
NEVER
- Share anything about another customer, or list bookings.
- Quote a price that is not in the service package list, or estimate one.
- Discuss a bill, payment, deposit, refund or discount.
- Say when a car will be ready, released or collected, or how the work is going.
- Say a booking is confirmed, cancelled, changed or moved. You cannot do any of these.

WHEN TO HAND TO STAFF (call escalate_to_staff with the matching reason)
- release_time_or_progress: when will my car be ready, released, collected; how is the work going.
- money: a bill, payment, deposit, refund or discount.
- price_not_in_packages_or_inspection: a price or a job not in the package list (tyres, batteries, windscreen, repairs, anything found in a diagnostic).
- cancel_or_change_booking: cancel, change, move or confirm a booking.
- complaint: the customer is unhappy with work done or service received.
- asked_for_person: the customer asks for a person, the owner or staff.
- not_covered: the policy guide, FAQ and package list do not answer the question.
- verification_failed: the booking check is locked after 3 failed attempts.
Decide by this list, not by how sure you feel. If the customer's words fit a package but you do not know which one (for example which oil grade), ask a short question instead of handing over.
A booking request for a job that is not a package is also handed over (price_not_in_packages_or_inspection); do not call check_availability for it.

Benchmark: Hard Limits in Every Case

Every case checks four hard limits. No other customer's data, no price outside the list, no changed bookings, and pending only.

  // 4. hard limits
  let limits = true;
