Badminton Court Booking Agent

HomeLive demoGitHub

A badminton centre takes court bookings by chat, and every booking needs a deposit. Staff answer the same questions about fees, holds and cancellations, and match payment screenshots to bookings by hand. Players must never see each other's bookings.

I built an AI assistant that answers only from the centre's policies and cites the clause behind every answer. It holds courts, reads payment receipts and checks them against the booking. Questions the policies do not settle go to staff with a summary they can act on.

Confirming payments, releasing courts and refunds stay with staff, one click each, and every click sends a message back to the player. The rules sit in the tool code, and the assistant was tested on 30 scenarios written before the build. Try the player chat and the staff dashboard side by side.

How It Works

  1. The player asks a question. Claude answers only from the three policies, and cites the clauses.
  2. To act, Claude calls a tool. The tool checks the rules in code before it saves anything.
  3. Payments, holds, cancellations and handovers wait for staff. Each staff click sends a message back to the player's chat.

Tools, Resources and Workflow

How Claude works

Claude reads the three policies at the start of each conversation. It must call a tool on every step, and the turn ends when it calls reply_to_player. Limits of 700 output tokens and 8 tool rounds stop a reply from looping.

Resource (read)Holds
policy://operationO1 to O21: the centre, hours, prices it may quote, facilities, house rules, payment, privacy.
policy://bookingB1 to B25: booking rules, fee and deposit, the 30-minute hold, confirmation, the booking check, receipts.
policy://cancellationC1 to C13: who can cancel, the refund, how a cancellation is processed.
Tool (act)Checked in code
check_availabilityToday and the next 7 days only (B1), opening hours (O3). Times under 2 hours away are not offered (B3). Free or taken only: no names (O18).
request_bookingWindow, 1 to 3 hours, 2 hours ahead, up to 2 courts at once per mobile (B1 to B4). Conflict re-checked at the moment of writing; nearest alternatives on slot_taken (B6). Fee from the fee table, RM 50 deposit, 30-minute hold, reference issued by code (B7, B8, B11).
verify_bookingReference and mobile last 4 must both match, compared as text (B17). Same error whether the booking is missing or the digits are wrong. Locked after 3 failed checks (B18).
get_bookingOnly a booking verified or made in this conversation. One booking, never a list (O20). No mobile number returned.
report_paymentNeeds an image uploaded in this message: "I have paid" is refused (B12); refused once the hold has expired (B13). Receipt fields compared with the booking in code; differences flagged for staff (B23). Records the receipt only: the booking stays held (B25).
quote_cancellationVerified, confirmed, and the session not started (C1, C3). Refund worked out in code: deposit plus any court fee paid at booking.
request_cancellationOnly after a quote, and only in a later message than the quote (C7). Pending until staff approve; the booking stays in place (C8).
hand_to_staffReason must be one of the 8 fixed reasons; check_failed only after the booking check has locked (B18). A structured summary for staff who cannot see the chat: root cause, clauses checked, amount, recommended action. Code attaches the player's words, the chat contact and, for check_failed, the checks tried.
reply_to_playerClause IDs checked against the clause list; links built by code, with the clauses an unclear handover names. Every reply states its policy case; not_covered and unclear are refused without the matching handover this turn. A reply that claims a handover, a policy gap or offers to pass it on is refused unless hand_to_staff ran this turn.

Security and Business Rules

PrincipleHow it is applied here
Policies as the only sourceThree policies with numbered clauses are MCP resources. Every answer cites the clauses it used, and code builds the links; a question no clause covers is marked "Not covered by policy".
Four kinds of questionEach question is covered, contradicts a rule, not covered, or unclear. Covered is answered, contradicts is declined, and the last two go to staff.
Rules in code, not in the promptThe booking window, the 30-minute hold, the deposit and the court limit are checked in the tools. A rule the assistant must never break has no prompt to talk it out of.
Actions the assistant cannot takeNo tool confirms, cancels, releases, refunds or lists bookings. Those are staff buttons, each sending a fixed message.
Booking check before any detailThe booking reference and the last 4 mobile digits must match. Three wrong tries lock the check and hand the enquiry to staff.
Payment only with a receipt"I have paid" is not a payment report. A receipt image is read, compared with the booking in code, and left for staff to confirm against the bank.
Named errorsEvery refusal has a name and a next step: offer the alternatives, ask again, or hand over.
Handovers staff can act onStaff cannot see the chat, so each handover carries the player's words, the contact, the root cause and a recommended action.
A reply cannot claim a handoverA reply that says staff will follow up, or that the policy is silent, is refused unless the handover was made.
Tested before trusted30 cases were written before the build and rerun after every change. Failures were fixed in code, and scorer fixes were checked against recorded runs.

Code Walkthrough

The Booking Check Lives in the Tool

No booking detail is shown until the reference and the last 4 mobile digits match. Three wrong tries lock the check for the conversation. The lookup returns one booking, never a list.

// tools.js: verify_booking and get_booking
      verify_booking({ booking_ref, mobile_last4 }, ctx) {
        if (session.failedChecks >= MAX_CHECKS)
          return fail("locked", "Booking check locked after 3 failed attempts (B18). Do not check again. Hand to staff with reason check_failed.", false);
        const last4 = String(mobile_last4 == null ? "" : mobile_last4).trim();
        if (!/^\d{4}$/.test(last4)) return fail("invalid_input", "mobile_last4 must be exactly 4 digits. Ask the player.", true);
        const ref = normRef(booking_ref);
        const b = find(store.load(), ref);
        if (!b || digits(b.mobile).slice(-4) !== last4) {
          session.failedChecks += 1;
          session.attempts.push({ booking_ref: ref, mobile_last4: last4, at: nowIso(), message: String((ctx && ctx.message) || "").trim() });
          const left = MAX_CHECKS - session.failedChecks;
          return fail(left > 0 ? "no_match" : "locked",
            left > 0 ? `The booking reference and mobile digits do not match. Share nothing about the booking. ${left} attempt(s) left (B17).`
                     : "The booking reference and mobile digits do not match. No attempts left (B18): hand to staff with reason check_failed.",
            left > 0);
        }
        if (!session.verified.includes(ref)) session.verified.push(ref);
        return { ok: true, verified: true, booking_ref: ref };
      },

      get_booking({ booking_ref }) {
        const ref = normRef(booking_ref);
        if (!canSee(ref)) return fail("not_verified", "This booking has not been verified in this conversation. Ask for the reference and the last 4 digits of the mobile, then call verify_booking (B17).", true);
        const state = store.load();
        const b = find(state, ref);
        const rc = state.receipts.filter((r) => r.booking_ref === ref).slice(-1)[0];
        const cx = state.cancellations.find((c) => c.booking_ref === ref && c.status === "pending");
        const out = {
          booking_ref: ref, status: b.status, court: b.court, date: b.date, day: LONG_DAYS[dayOfWeek(b.date)],
          start: hhmm(b.start_hour), end: hhmm(b.start_hour + b.hours), hours: b.hours,
          court_fee_rm: b.court_fee_rm, deposit_rm: b.deposit_rm,
          paid_rm: { deposit: b.paid.deposit_rm, court_fee: b.paid.court_fee_rm },
          court_fee_due_at_counter_rm: b.status === "confirmed" ? b.court_fee_rm - b.paid.court_fee_rm : null,
        };
        if (b.status === "held") {
          out.amount_to_pay_now_rm = b.amount_due_now_rm;
          out.hold_expires_at = b.hold_expires_at.slice(11);
          out.hold_expired = !rc && toMin(nowIso()) > toMin(b.hold_expires_at);
          out.receipt_uploaded = !!rc;
        }
        if (cx) out.cancellation_pending = true;
        return { ok: true, booking: out };
      },

A Handover Staff Can Act On

Staff work from a queue and cannot see the chat. The tool requires a root cause and a recommended action, and code adds the player's words, the contact and the checks tried. A failed-check handover is refused until the check has actually locked.

// tools.js: hand_to_staff
      hand_to_staff(input, ctx) {
        if (!HANDOVER_REASONS.includes(input.reason))
          return fail("invalid_reason", "reason must be one of: " + HANDOVER_REASONS.join(", "), true);
        const rootCause = String(input.root_cause || "").trim();
        const action = String(input.recommended_action || "").trim();
        if (rootCause.length < 15 || action.length < 10)
          return fail("missing_details", "Staff cannot see this chat. Give root_cause (why it needs a person) and recommended_action (the next step for staff).", true);
        const checked = (Array.isArray(input.clause_ids) ? input.clause_ids : []).map((x) => String(x).trim().toUpperCase()).filter((x) => clauses[x]);
        if (input.reason === "policy_unclear" && !checked.length)
          return fail("missing_details", "For policy_unclear, name the clause that seemed to apply in clause_ids.", true);
        if (input.reason === "check_failed" && session.failedChecks < MAX_CHECKS)
          return fail("not_locked", "check_failed is for a booking check locked after 3 failures (B18). The check is not locked; ask the player to check the reference and digits.", false);
        const tried = input.reason === "check_failed" ? session.attempts.slice() : [];
        const ref = input.booking_ref ? normRef(input.booking_ref) : (tried.length ? tried[tried.length - 1].booking_ref : "");
        const state = store.load();
        const id = "HO-" + String(state.handovers.length + 1).padStart(3, "0");
        state.handovers.push({
          handover_id: id, reason: input.reason, created_at: nowIso(), status: "open",
          // For a failed check, the player's request is the message that started the checks.
          question: (tried.length && tried[0].message) || String((ctx && ctx.message) || "").trim(), contact: contactOf(ctx),
          player_name: input.player_name || "", booking_ref: ref,
          root_cause: rootCause, clause_ids: checked, amount_rm: typeof input.amount_rm === "number" ? input.amount_rm : null,
          checks_tried: tried, recommended_action: action, told_player: "", replies: [],
        });
        store.save(state);
        (session.handoversByTurn[ctx.turn] = session.handoversByTurn[ctx.turn] || []).push(input.reason);
        if (input.reason === "policy_unclear") (session.unclearClausesByTurn[ctx.turn] = session.unclearClausesByTurn[ctx.turn] || []).push(...checked);
        made(ctx, "handover", id);
        return { ok: true, handover_id: id, message: "Handed to staff with the summary. Tell the player staff will follow up here. Do not promise a time." };
      },

Every Reply Is Checked

Every reply states its policy case. A not-covered or unclear reply, or one that claims or offers a handover, is refused unless the handover was made. Citations come from the clause list.

// tools.js: reply_to_player
      reply_to_player(input, ctx) {
        const message = String(input.message || "").trim();
        if (!message) return fail("invalid_input", "message is empty.", true);
        const asked = Array.isArray(input.clause_ids) ? input.clause_ids : [];
        const ids = [], dropped = [];
        asked.forEach((x) => {
          const id = String(x).trim().toUpperCase();
          if (clauses[id]) { if (!ids.includes(id)) ids.push(id); } else dropped.push(String(x));
        });
        const turnHandovers = session.handoversByTurn[ctx.turn] || [];
        if (!POLICY_CASES.includes(input.policy_case))
          return fail("invalid_input", "policy_case must be one of: " + POLICY_CASES.join(", "), true);
        const needs = CASE_NEEDS[input.policy_case];
        if (needs && !turnHandovers.includes(needs))
          return fail("handover_not_made", `A ${input.policy_case} question goes to staff: call hand_to_staff with reason ${needs}${needs === "policy_unclear" ? " and clause_id" : ""} first, then reply. Do not offer to pass it on; pass it on.`, true);
        if (HANDOVER_OFFER.test(message) && !turnHandovers.length)
          return fail("handover_not_made", "Do not offer to pass the question to staff. If it belongs with staff (not covered, unclear, or another handover reason), call hand_to_staff now, then reply; otherwise answer without the offer.", true);
        // A reply may say staff will take it up only if the handover was actually made this turn.
        if (HANDOVER_CLAIM.test(message) && !turnHandovers.length)
          return fail("handover_not_made", "The reply says the enquiry goes to staff, but hand_to_staff was not called in this turn. Call hand_to_staff with the matching reason first, then reply.", true);
        // A reply that says the policies do not settle the question must come with the handover (four-way rule).
        if (POLICY_GAP_CLAIM.test(message) && !turnHandovers.length)
          return fail("handover_not_made", "The reply says the policy does not settle the question. Then it is not covered (hand_to_staff reason not_in_policy) or unclear (reason policy_unclear, with clause_id). Call hand_to_staff first; do not offer to ask or suggest the player asks.", true);
        // The player is cited the same clauses the unclear handover names for staff.
        (session.unclearClausesByTurn[ctx.turn] || []).forEach((id) => { if (!ids.includes(id)) ids.push(id); });
        const notCovered = turnHandovers.some((r) => NOT_COVERED_REASONS.includes(r));
        const citations = notCovered ? [] : ids.map((id) => ({ id, policy: clauses[id].policy, page: clauses[id].page, label: `${clauses[id].policy}, Clause ${id}` }));
        const line = notCovered ? "Not covered by policy" : citations.map((c) => c.label).join(" · ");
        const items = session.madeByTurn[ctx.turn] || [];
        if (items.length) {
          const state = store.load();
          const told = message + (line ? "\n" + line : "");
          const where = { handover: ["handovers", "handover_id"], receipt: ["receipts", "receipt_id"], cancellation: ["cancellations", "cancellation_id"], booking: ["bookings", "booking_ref"] };
          items.forEach(({ kind, id }) => { const [k, f] = where[kind]; const x = state[k].find((y) => y[f] === id); if (x) x.told_player = told; });
          store.save(state);
        }
        return { ok: true, policy_case: input.policy_case, reply: message, citation_line: line, citations, not_covered: notCovered, dropped_clause_ids: dropped };
      },
    };

Staff Actions Send Fixed Messages

Confirm, Not received, Release and Approve are staff buttons, not tools. Each one sends a message written in code to the player's chat.

// tools.js: STAFF
  const STAFF = {
    confirm(state, ref, now) {
      const b = state.bookings.find((x) => x.booking_ref === ref);
      if (!b || b.status !== "held") return fail("not_held", "Only a held booking can be confirmed.", false);
      b.status = "confirmed"; b.confirmed_at = now;
      b.paid = { deposit_rm: b.deposit_rm, court_fee_rm: b.pay_fee_now ? b.court_fee_rm : 0 };
      state.receipts.filter((r) => r.booking_ref === ref && r.status === "to_check").forEach((r) => (r.status = "confirmed"));
      const due = b.court_fee_rm - b.paid.court_fee_rm;
      return { ok: true, message: pushMessage(state, ref, "confirmed",
        `Booking confirmed: ${ref}, ${sessionText(b)}. Amount paid ${rm(b.amount_due_now_rm)}. ` +
        (due > 0 ? `Court fee ${rm(due)} is due at the counter after the session.` : "Court fee paid in full.") +
        " Please check in at the counter at least 10 minutes before the session.", now) };
    },
    notReceived(state, ref, now) {
      const b = state.bookings.find((x) => x.booking_ref === ref);
      if (!b || b.status !== "held") return fail("not_held", "Only a held booking can be marked not received.", false);
      b.status = "released";
      state.receipts.filter((r) => r.booking_ref === ref && r.status === "to_check").forEach((r) => (r.status = "not_received"));
      return { ok: true, message: pushMessage(state, ref, "not_received",
        `Payment not received: we could not find the payment for ${ref} (${sessionText(b)}) in our bank record, so the court has been released. Please contact the counter if you have paid.`, now) };
    },
    release(state, ref, now) {
      const b = state.bookings.find((x) => x.booking_ref === ref);
      if (!b || b.status !== "held") return fail("not_held", "Only a held booking can be released.", false);
      b.status = "released";
      return { ok: true, message: pushMessage(state, ref, "released",
        `Hold expired: no payment was reported within 30 minutes for ${ref} (${sessionText(b)}), so the court has been released. Nothing has been charged.`, now) };
    },
    approveCancellation(state, id, now) {
      const c = state.cancellations.find((x) => x.cancellation_id === id);
      if (!c || c.status !== "pending") return fail("not_pending", "No pending cancellation with that ID.", false);
      const b = state.bookings.find((x) => x.booking_ref === c.booking_ref);
      c.status = "approved"; c.approved_at = now; b.status = "cancelled";
      const fee = c.refund.court_fee_rm > 0 ? ` and the court fee of ${rm(c.refund.court_fee_rm)} paid at booking` : "";
      return { ok: true, message: pushMessage(state, b.booking_ref, "cancelled",
        `Cancellation approved: ${b.booking_ref} (${sessionText(b)}) is cancelled and the court released. Your deposit of ${rm(c.refund.deposit_rm)}${fee} will be refunded to the account that paid within 3 working days.`, now) };
    },

The Agent Loop

Claude must call a tool on every step, and the turn ends at reply_to_player. Tool errors go back with their category. Limits stop a runaway turn.

// agent.js: runTurn
    for (let round = 0; round < limits.maxToolRounds; round++) {
      let res = await call(AUTO_ONLY.has(args.model) ? "auto" : "any");
      if (!res.ok) {
        const body = await res.text();
        if (res.status === 400 && /tool_choice/.test(body) && !AUTO_ONLY.has(args.model)) {
          AUTO_ONLY.add(args.model);
          res = await call("auto");
          if (!res.ok) throw new Error(`Anthropic API ${res.status}: ${(await res.text()).slice(0, 300)}`);
        } else throw new Error(`Anthropic API ${res.status}: ${body.slice(0, 300)}`);
      }
      const data = await res.json();
      Object.keys(usage).forEach((k) => (usage[k] += (data.usage && data.usage[k]) || 0));
      args.messages.push({ role: "assistant", content: data.content });

      const calls = data.content.filter((b) => b.type === "tool_use");
      if (!calls.length) {
        // A plain-text ending (possible on "auto"): send it back once to go through reply_to_player.
        if (!nudged) {
          nudged = true;
          trace.push({ type: "note", text: "Plain-text ending sent back: replies go through reply_to_player." });
          args.messages.push({ role: "user", content: USE_REPLY_TOOL });
          continue;
        }
        args.messages.push({ role: "user", content: "Continue." });
        args.messages.push({ role: "assistant", content: [{ type: "text", text: FALLBACK }] });
        trace.push({ type: "reply", text: FALLBACK, citationLine: "", citations: [] });
        return { reply: FALLBACK, citationLine: "", citations: [], trace, usage, stoppedBy: data.stop_reason };
      }
      const results = [];
      let final = null;
      for (const block of calls) {
        const result = allowed.has(block.name)
          ? await args.callTool(block.name, block.input)
          : { ok: false, error: { category: "unknown_tool", retryable: false, message: `No tool named ${block.name}.` } };
        trace.push({ type: "tool", name: block.name, input: block.input, result });
        results.push({ type: "tool_result", tool_use_id: block.id, content: JSON.stringify(result), is_error: result && result.ok === false });
        if (block.name === "reply_to_player" && result.ok) final = result;
      }
      args.messages.push({ role: "user", content: results });
      if (final) {
        // The reply the player sees is the tool's output; record it as the assistant's turn.
        const shown = final.reply + (final.citation_line ? "\n\n" + final.citation_line : "");
        args.messages.push({ role: "assistant", content: [{ type: "text", text: shown }] });
        trace.push({ type: "reply", text: final.reply, citationLine: final.citation_line, citations: final.citations, notCovered: final.not_covered });
        return { reply: final.reply, citationLine: final.citation_line, citations: final.citations, trace, usage, stoppedBy: "reply_to_player" };
      }
    }

The Four-Way Rule

The prompt tells Claude how to classify each question before it answers. The tool checks then hold it to that choice.

// prompt.js: the policies settle every rule question
THE POLICIES SETTLE EVERY RULE QUESTION
Answer only from the Operation, Booking and Cancellation policies below. For each question, decide which of four cases it is:
1. Covered: a clause answers it. Answer and cite the clause.
2. Contradicts: a clause forbids what the player asks. Decline, state the rule, offer what the rule does allow. Make no exception.
3. Not covered: no clause speaks to it. Do not answer yes or no and do not guess. hand_to_staff(reason "not_in_policy").
4. Unclear: a clause seems to apply but neither allows nor forbids this case, or the answer turns on something the clause does not define (who counts, when a deadline or a status begins). Do not pick a reading, do not ask for details first, and do not explain the general rule as if it answered the case. Call hand_to_staff(reason "policy_unclear", with the clause in clause_ids), then say the policy does not settle it, naming the clause.
Telling 1 from 4: answer "covered" only if a clause states the outcome for this exact situation. If you would have to infer it (combine two clauses, read between them, or decide what a term means for this case), it is unclear.
Telling 3 from 4: if you can name a clause that touches the question (a limit, a list, a deadline, a definition) but it does not settle this case, it is unclear, not not_covered. not_covered is for topics no clause touches.
Decide by the clauses, not by how sure you feel. In cases 3 and 4 the hand_to_staff call comes BEFORE reply_to_player: never tell the player their question was passed to staff unless you called hand_to_staff in this turn.
A declined request (case 2) is not handed to staff: the decline is the answer. A request to refund a no-show deposit is declined under C5 and C11; it is not a refund dispute (C13).

Benchmark: Hard Limits in Every Case

Every case checks the hard limits and the handover summary. Nothing confirmed or refunded by the assistant, no other player's details, no price outside the policies, existing bookings untouched.
