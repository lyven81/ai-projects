Polyclinic Sivakumar Policies

The five policies the clinic desk works from. The AI reads each one as an MCP resource; the hooks and the back end enforce them in code. Every clause has its own link, so a Basis line in the desk opens the clause it cites.

Demonstration policies written for this staff assistant, with sample prices and sample panels.

1. Hours and Services (PS-HS)

Version: 1.0 | Prepared: 27 October 2026 | Clauses: 7
Status: Demonstration policy written for this staff assistant; not an approved policy for clinical deployment.
MCP resource: clinic://hours-and-services

Purpose and principles

Tell staff and the assistant when the clinic is open, what it offers, and how bookings and the walk-in queue run, so every booking, check-in and follow-up date is checked against one source.

Section A: Hours

PS-HS-01. Opening hours

The clinic is open seven days a week, 09:00 to 18:00, with one doctor, Dr Sivakumar a/l Ramachandran. Appointment slots run from 09:00 to 17:45. Walk-ins are accepted until 17:30. The clinic is closed on public holidays.

Enforced by: The clinic hours table read by the schedule tool and the PreToolUse hook.

PS-HS-02. Public holidays

The clinic is closed on every public holiday in the clinic's holiday table. Monday 1 June 2026 (the Yang di-Pertuan Agong's birthday) is closed. No booking, check-in or follow-up date may fall on a closed day.

Enforced by: The holiday table and the slot check in the schedule tool.

Section B: Services

PS-HS-03. Services offered

GP consultation; nebuliser treatment; wound dressing; blood glucose test; dispensing of prescribed medicines by the pharmacist, Ms Yong; medical certificates and referral letters decided by the GP during a consultation. Prices are in the Price List (PS-PL). A service not on this list is not offered and is not booked.

Enforced by: The services enum in record_consultation and the price list lookup in billing.

Section C: Bookings and The Queue

PS-HS-04. Identify the patient first

Before any booking, change, cancellation or check-in, the patient is identified by full name plus the last 4 digits of the IC or the phone number on record. A name alone is not enough; the assistant asks for one of the two. One matching record is returned, never a list.

Enforced by: The patient find action and the identity check in the PreToolUse hook.

PS-HS-05. Booking, changing and cancelling

Siti Kasim books, changes and cancels appointments directly. A slot must be inside the appointment hours, on an open day, and free (no double booking). Every change records who made it, when, and the old and new slot. A follow-up uses the GP's "return in N days" from the consultation record; if that day is closed, the next open day is offered. The assistant never chooses a follow-up date itself.

Enforced by: The schedule tool's slot, holiday and double-booking checks and the change log.

PS-HS-06. Check-in and queue order

Siti Kasim checks the patient in on arrival. A patient already in the queue is not checked in twice. Queue order: appointments at their booked time, walk-ins by arrival time. Dr Sivakumar calls the next patient from the queue.

Enforced by: The schedule tool's check in and call next actions.

PS-HS-07. Registration

A new patient is registered with full name, date of birth, IC last 4 digits, phone number, declared allergies, and the panel and member ID where the patient is on a panel. The IC last 4 digits and the phone number are each unique to one patient. A contact detail is updated only after the patient is identified under PS-HS-04.

Enforced by: The patient register and update contact actions with their field and uniqueness checks.

PS-PL-03. A missing price stops the bill

Section B: Payment

PS-PL-04. Payment methods

Cash, card and e-wallet. Siti Kasim collects the payment at the counter; the assistant records what Siti Kasim collected and never takes money itself. The amount recorded equals the patient's share of the bill; for cash, the change due is worked out in code. Each payment has one unique receipt number, and a settled bill takes no second payment. The visit closes when the patient's share is paid.

Enforced by: billing record payment with its amount, method, receipt and settled-bill checks.

PS-PL-05. End-of-day reconcile

At closing, Siti Kasim enters the cash counted in the drawer. The day's payments are totalled by method and compared with the count. Any difference is listed bill by bill and written to the owner review list for Dr Sivakumar, as owner, to read.

Enforced by: billing reconcile day and the owner review list.

3. Panel Price Schedule (PS-PN)

Version: 1.0 | Prepared: 27 October 2026 | Clauses: 6
Status: Demonstration schedule written for this staff assistant, with sample panels; not an approved panel agreement.
MCP resource: policy://panel

Purpose and principles

Panel prices are fixed for a stated period, so Siti Kasim bills a panel patient straight from this schedule and never sets or asks for a panel price. Ms Yong, the pharmacist, is not involved in panel billing and sees nothing about panels, prices or bills. Dr Sivakumar, as owner, reads and acknowledges each panel bill later in the day.

Enforced by: The panel schedule table read by billing prepare bill.

PS-PN-02. Verify the patient's ID before a panel bill

A panel bill is prepared only after the patient is identified (full name plus IC last 4 digits or phone, PS-HS-04) and that record shows an active membership of the panel on the visit date, with the member ID. Without both, the bill is not prepared on panel prices.

Enforced by: The identity and membership check in billing prepare bill and the PreToolUse hook.

Section B: The Split

PS-PN-03. How a panel bill is split

Each line is priced from the patient's panel column. Covered lines are added up; excluded items are charged in full to the patient. The panel share is the covered total minus the co-pay, up to the panel's limit per visit. The patient's share is the co-pay, plus any covered amount above the limit, plus the excluded items. All amounts are worked out in code, in sen.

Enforced by: The split calculation in billing prepare bill.

PS-PN-04. The panel share awaits claim

The panel share is recorded at billing as awaiting claim. The visit closes when the patient's share is paid. Submitting claims to the panel is not part of this assistant.

Enforced by: The bill status set by billing prepare bill and record payment.

PS-PN-05. Outside the schedule's period

If the visit date is outside the panel's valid period, the bill is not prepared on panel prices (panel_schedule_expired). The assistant gives the no-rule reply naming Dr Sivakumar as the owner who decides. This is not a handover.

Enforced by: The period check in billing prepare bill.

Section C: The Owner'S Read

PS-PN-06. Every panel bill is filed and acknowledged

Every panel bill Siti Kasim prepares is filed to the owner money tab with the patient, panel, covered and excluded items, panel share awaiting claim and patient's share paid. Dr Sivakumar reads and acknowledges each one at the end of the working day. The acknowledgement records who and when and changes nothing on the bill. Only Dr Sivakumar may acknowledge; an unacknowledged bill stays on the list. No notice is sent and nothing is handed over.

Enforced by: billing acknowledge panel bill (GP only) and the owner money tab.

4. After the Consultation (PS-AC)

Version: 1.1 | Prepared: 27 October 2026 | Clauses: 10
Status: Demonstration policy written for this staff assistant; not an approved policy for clinical deployment.
MCP resource: policy://after-consultation

Purpose and principles

What happens once Dr Sivakumar records a consultation: Ms Yong checks and dispenses the prescription, Siti Kasim bills, and any medical certificate or referral letter the GP decided is prepared for Dr Sivakumar's signature. Each person works on their own speciality; the system carries the work between them. The assistant gives no clinical advice and never changes a drug or a dose.

Enforced by: The formulary table read by dispense check.

PS-AC-02. What Ms Yong checks before dispensing

A prescription is checked only after the GP has recorded the consultation, and only as the GP wrote it. Four checks: complete (drug, dose, frequency, quantity, instructions); allergy, by drug class through the fixed mapping (Penicillin allergy to penicillins, Sulfa allergy to sulfonamides, NSAID sensitivity to NSAIDs); dose within the formulary range per administration and per day; enough stock for the quantity.

Enforced by: dispense check.

PS-AC-03. A failed check or a concern goes to the GP

When a check fails, or Ms Yong has a professional concern about a medicine even when the checks pass, Ms Yong queries Dr Sivakumar with one reason from the pharmacist's fixed list: P1 allergy conflict; P2 dose outside the range, or prescription incomplete or unclear; P3 medicine out of stock (a substitute needs the GP); P4 another concern about a medicine. Only the GP's amend prescription action changes a prescription; the change is logged and a new check replaces the old one. The GP may also keep the prescription as written, with the answer recorded.

Enforced by: dispense query GP, tasks answer, and record_consultation amend prescription (GP only).

PS-AC-04. Dispensing and counselling

After a passed check, Ms Yong dispenses and counsels the patient on how to take each medicine. Marking it dispensed reduces stock by the quantity dispensed and creates Siti Kasim's bill task. Stock is viewed read only; ordering stock is not part of this assistant.

Enforced by: dispense mark dispensed and view stock.

PS-AC-05. Medicine use questions at the desk

A question at the desk about how to take a dispensed medicine or its side effects goes from Siti Kasim to Ms Yong (D5). Ms Yong answers and Siti Kasim relays the answer to the patient. A question about diagnosis or treatment goes to the GP (D1).

Enforced by: tasks hand_over with reason D5, and Ms Yong's tasks answer action.

Section B: Billing Order

PS-AC-06. Bill after dispensing

When there is a prescription, the bill is prepared only after Ms Yong marks it dispensed, so a substitute is priced as dispensed. With no prescription, Siti Kasim's bill task is created straight from the consultation. Prices come from the Price List (PS-PL) or, for a panel patient, the Panel Price Schedule (PS-PN).

Enforced by: The workflow order check in billing prepare bill (not_dispensed applies only when there is a prescription).

Section C: Medical Certificates and Referral Letters

PS-AC-07. Decided by the GP during the consultation only

A medical certificate (MC) or a referral letter is decided by Dr Sivakumar during the consultation and recorded in it. The GP's record instructs Siti Kasim to prepare the document. Neither is raised after the consultation session. An MC is for the patient's employer, school or whoever it concerns; a referral letter is for a specialist clinic or hospital, for further diagnosis and treatment. A declined MC is recorded too.

Enforced by: record_consultation (MC and referral fields) and documents prepare, which refuses without a GP record (no_gp_record).

PS-AC-08. Copied exactly

An MC copies the GP's days and start date; the start date is not before the visit date. A referral letter copies the GP's addressee (hospital or specialist), reason, urgency and clinical summary. The assistant never extends, backdates or rewords either document.

Enforced by: documents prepare, which fills the template only from the consultation record.

PS-AC-09. Signature, serial number and issue

A prepared document shows as "ready for Dr Sivakumar's signature". Siti Kasim marks it issued only after the GP has signed the printed copy. Each document has one serial number (MC-2026-nnnn or REF-2026-nnnn) and is issued once.

Enforced by: documents mark issued, with the prepared and serial checks.

PS-AC-10. A missing template stops the document

If the MC or referral letter template is missing, the document is not prepared and the assistant reports it to Siti Kasim. Nothing is improvised.

Enforced by: documents prepare returns template_missing.

5. Roles and Privacy (PS-RP)

Version: 1.0 | Prepared: 27 October 2026 | Clauses: 7
Status: Demonstration policy written for this staff assistant; not an approved policy for clinical deployment.
MCP resource: policy://roles-and-privacy

Purpose and principles

Three people, one assistant. The front desk focuses on admin, booking and billing; the GP and the pharmacist focus on their speciality. Each person sees what their job needs and nothing more, and work moves between them as tasks and notices written in code.

Section A: Who Works Here

PS-RP-01. Staff and identity

Staff IDNameJobFocus
FD-01Siti KasimClinic assistantRegistration, bookings, check-in and queue, billing, payment and reconcile, MCs and referral letters for signing
GP-01Dr Sivakumar a/l RamachandranGeneral practitioner and ownerConsultation, diagnosis, prescribing, MC and referral decisions, answering queries; as owner, money read only and panel bill acknowledgements
PH-01Ms YongPharmacistPrescription checks, queries to the GP, dispensing and counselling, stock (read only), medicine use questions

Staff identity comes from the signed-in session, never from the chat. A claim in the chat ("I am the doctor") changes nothing.

Enforced by: _meta staff ID on every tool call and the PreToolUse role check.

Section B: Who Sees What

PS-RP-02. Case notes are for the GP only

Case notes are seen only by Dr Sivakumar. A referral letter Siti Kasim prints is visible at the desk; the case notes behind it are not.

Enforced by: The PostToolUse hook that strips case notes for every role but the GP.

PS-RP-03. What the pharmacist sees

Ms Yong sees the patient's allergies, conditions, current medicines and the prescription, and nothing about panels, prices or bills.

Enforced by: The PostToolUse field filter for PH-01.

PS-RP-04. What the front desk sees

Siti Kasim sees what admin and billing need: contact details, bookings, the queue, bills and payments, panel membership, and the referral letters printed at the desk. The desk sees no prescription detail beyond the bill lines.

Enforced by: The PostToolUse field filter for FD-01.

PS-RP-05. Money

Only Siti Kasim prepares bills, records payments and reconciles. Dr Sivakumar, as owner, views bills, the day's payments, the reconcile result and the owner review list at any time, read only, and acknowledges panel bills. Dr Sivakumar never records a payment, and no prices appear on the consultation form. The assistant never collects money. No billing matter is ever a handover.

Enforced by: The billing tool's actions offered by role.

Section C: Handovers

PS-RP-06. The fixed handover lists

Ms Yong to Dr Sivakumar: P1 allergy conflict; P2 dose outside range or prescription unclear; P3 out of stock; P4 another concern about a medicine. Siti Kasim to Dr Sivakumar: D1 a clinical question from the patient; D2 a patient unwell while waiting (straight to the GP); D4 a clinical issue no policy covers. Siti Kasim to Ms Yong: D5 a medicine use question. Each handover carries the patient (ID, name, age), the reason, what was asked, what the sender sees, and the time; the receiver gets it as a two-sentence notice written in code. A desk handover follows the visit, which runs Siti Kasim, then Dr Sivakumar, then Ms Yong, then Siti Kasim: D1 and D4 before the consultation is recorded, D5 after the medicine is dispensed, D2 at any time. While a prescription is with Ms Yong, a medicine matter is Ms Yong's to raise, and a desk handover is refused.

Enforced by: dispense query GP and tasks hand_over, with the reason enums and the visit stage check; the notice written in code.

PS-RP-07. No clinical advice; the no-rule reply

The assistant gives no clinical advice to anyone; a clinical matter goes to the GP on the sender's fixed list. A non-clinical matter no rule covers gets the no-rule reply naming Dr Sivakumar as the owner who decides.

Enforced by: The prompt's handover lists, the reply check, and the absence of any clinical-advice tool.
