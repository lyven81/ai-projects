# Wee Auto Car Care: frequently asked questions

**When are you open?**
Monday to Saturday, 10:00 to 18:00. Closed on Sundays and we do not take bookings for Sundays.

**Do I need a booking, or can I walk in?**
A booking is recommended so a bay and a qualified mechanic are free for you. Walk-ins are served when a bay and a mechanic are both free.

**How often should I change engine oil?**
Mineral oil: about every 5,000 km. Semi-synthetic: about every 7,500 km. Fully synthetic: about every 10,000 km. Follow your car's manual if it says otherwise.

**Which engine oil grade should I choose?**
Check your owner's manual for the grade (for example 5W-30 or 0W-20). Many newer petrol cars use 0W-20 or 5W-30. If you are not sure, our mechanic will confirm when you arrive.

**How long does an oil change take?**
About 30 minutes.

**How long does a wheel alignment take?**
About one hour.

**Can I wait at the workshop?**
Yes, there is a waiting area with seating.

**What should I bring?**
The car and its key. For a wheel alignment, please make sure the tyres are not badly worn.

**When should I service the air-cond?**
If the air is not cold or smells musty, an air-cond service is recommended. A gas top-up helps when cooling is weak but there is no leak.

**Do you replace tyres and batteries?**
Yes. The price depends on the tyre size and brand, or the battery model, so our staff will quote it.

**What payment methods do you take?**
Staff handle all payment questions.
