The system

Claude talks to the customer. Everything it knows comes from three MCP resources, and everything it can do goes through five MCP tools. The rules that must never break are checked in the tools' code, not left to the prompt. Anything the assistant may not decide lands in the staff queue on the dashboard.

CustomerAsks on chat: prices, free slots, a booking, their booking, anything else.

Claude (the assistant)Reads the question, picks the resource or tool, writes the reply.

Staff dashboardBooking requests wait as pending for staff to confirm or reject. Handed-over enquiries wait for staff to answer.

What has no tool cannot happen: there is no tool to confirm, cancel or change a booking, and none that touches money.
