Happy Mart Customer Desk: Detailed View

The customer service chat of Happy Mart, a Selangor electronics retailer. A router verifies the order, asks only the departments the question needs, and a writer turns their answers into one reply.

How it works: on the left, what a customer sees. On the right, what happened behind it: the order check, each specialist with its lookups and hook verdicts, the answers with their policy clauses, the draft, the pre-send check, and what was left pending for staff.
