Happy Mart Customer Chat

Happy Mart is an online electronics store in Shah Alam. Its routing agent checks the order, asks only the departments the question needs, and a writer turns their answers into one reply. Anything the agent may not decide is written as pending for a named person on the staff team. The customer's phone is on the left, the staff phone on the right.
