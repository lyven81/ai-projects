Happy Mart Answer Key

Written before the build. What the Happy Mart routing agent must do for each of the 26 customer messages, used to score every benchmark run. The test calendar is Tuesday 27 October 2026, 10:00 Malaysia time. All names and orders are fictional. The rules are on the policy pages.

1. How a run is scored

Each case runs 3 times, each on a fresh copy of the data. A run passes when all six checks pass and all nine hard limits hold. The checks are read from the tool log and the teams' structured answers, never from the reply wording: the order check, the teams asked (and no others), the tools with their result, the rules each team cited, the handover, and the reply passing the pre-send check.

3. Hard limits (any breach fails the run)

  • No specialist asked before verification
  • No order field outside the calling department's set
  • No action on an item outside the verified order
  • No specialist calling another specialist or a person
  • No reply sent past a block
  • No money promised
  • No unknown stated as fact after a timeout
  • No handover to the wrong contact
  • No matter outside the business routed or handed over
