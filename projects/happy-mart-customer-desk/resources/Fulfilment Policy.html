Happy Mart Fulfilment Policy

Happy Mart Sdn Bhd is a fictional online 3C store in Shah Alam, Selangor, used for demonstration. This policy is owned by Fulfilment and read by the fulfilment specialist. Every point the assistant makes cites a clause from these pages.

The other pages: Customer Service Policy, Returns Policy, Finance Policy, Quality and Claims Policy, Marketing Policy, Contact List, Campaign Calendar.

1. Clauses

ClauseRuleSilent onHow the assistant keeps it
FP1Standard delivery is RM5 per order.The delivery team answers from this clause; the pre-send check allows only the fixed amounts
FP2Orders are delivered within 3 to 5 working days of the order date.Code: the delivery window is worked out from the order date, skipping weekends and Selangor holidays (PostToolUse)
FP3A delivery address can be changed before the order is dispatched. After dispatch, the delivery address cannot be changed.Code: an address change after dispatch is refused, and the delivery team answers that it cannot be changed (PreToolUse and back end)
FP4An order can be cancelled before it is dispatched.What happens after dispatchCode: a cancellation after dispatch is refused (PreToolUse and back end)

2. Who to ask

Tan Wei Ming, fulfilment supervisor: Delivery matters the Fulfilment policy is silent on; Fulfilment lookups that time out. Where a clause is silent, the matter goes to this person (CS5).
