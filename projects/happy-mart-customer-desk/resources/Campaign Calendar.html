Happy Mart Campaign Calendar

Happy Mart Sdn Bhd is a fictional online 3C store in Shah Alam, Selangor, used for demonstration. The campaign days set the free-delivery threshold and the sale items; the public holidays are skipped when the delivery window is worked out.
