Happy Mart Returns Policy

Happy Mart Sdn Bhd is a fictional online 3C store in Shah Alam, Selangor, used for demonstration. This policy is owned by Returns and read by the returns specialist. Every point the assistant makes cites a clause from these pages.

The other pages: Customer Service Policy, Fulfilment Policy, Finance Policy, Quality and Claims Policy, Marketing Policy, Contact List, Campaign Calendar.

ClauseRuleSilent onHow the assistant keeps it

2. Who to ask

Siti Hajar Kamal, returns lead: Return matters the Returns policy is silent on; approving returns after checking the item. Where a clause is silent, the matter goes to this person (CS5).
