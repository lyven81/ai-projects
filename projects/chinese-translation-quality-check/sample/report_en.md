## Part B: Additional information required by the ACE Market Listing Requirements

## B1. Review of performance

[17] For the first quarter ended 31 March 2026 ("1Q FYE2026"), the Group recorded revenue of RM86.42 million, which increased by RM15.07 million or 21.12% from RM71.35 million in the first quarter ended 31 March 2025 ("1Q FYE2025").

[18] All three operating segments contributed to the growth in revenue. Higher e-commerce parcel volumes raised last-mile delivery revenue, while new warehousing contracts and the Shah Alam warehouse added to warehousing and distribution revenue.

[19] Gross profit increased by 13.83% to RM21.61 million. However, the gross profit margin decreased from 26.60% to 25.00%, mainly due to start-up costs of the Shah Alam warehouse and higher diesel costs during the quarter.

[20] The Group delivered approximately 6.8 million parcels through its last-mile delivery network during the quarter. The new 250,000 square feet warehouse in Shah Alam began operations in February 2026, raising the Group's total warehouse space to approximately 1.2 million square feet.

[21] Profit before taxation increased by 24.45% to RM12.96 million, and the profit before taxation margin improved from 14.60% to 15.00%. The improvement was mainly due to lower finance costs after part of the proceeds from the initial public offering was used to repay a bridging loan.

[22] Profit after taxation increased by 24.43% to RM9.85 million, with the profit after taxation margin improving from 11.10% to 11.40%.

## B2. Comparison with the immediate preceding quarter

[23] Compared with the immediate preceding quarter ended 31 December 2025, Group revenue decreased by RM5.31 million or 5.79% from RM91.73 million to RM86.42 million.

[24] The decrease was mainly due to lower parcel and freight volumes after the fourth-quarter peak of year-end online sales, and fewer working days during the quarter because of the Chinese New Year and Hari Raya Aidilfitri holidays.

[25] In line with the lower revenue, gross profit decreased by 9.41% to RM21.61 million and profit before taxation decreased by 8.58% to RM12.96 million.

[26] Profit after taxation decreased by 3.80% to RM9.85 million. The smaller decrease in profit after taxation reflects a lower effective tax rate in the current quarter compared with the immediate preceding quarter.

## B3. Prospects

[27] Teras Laluan expects demand for logistics services in Malaysia to be supported by continued growth in online retail and in regional trade through Port Klang.

[28] The Group will continue to expand its warehousing capacity and delivery network in a measured manner, with priority given to contracts with longer tenures and to locations near major distribution routes.

[29] The Shah Alam warehouse is expected to contribute more fully to revenue in the coming quarters as its occupancy increases.

[30] The Group remains mindful of the risks to its operating environment. The rationalisation of diesel subsidies may increase transport costs. The availability of qualified drivers remains tight, and uncertainty over global tariffs may affect freight volumes.

[31] Barring unforeseen circumstances, the Board expects the performance of the Group for the financial year ending 31 December 2026 to remain satisfactory.
