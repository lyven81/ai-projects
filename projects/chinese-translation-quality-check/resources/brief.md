# Brief

Review the Chinese version of a listed company's quarterly report against the English original, for the investor relations team before release. The English is the authority. The review does not retranslate: it finds where the Chinese departs from the English or from the house style, says why, and proposes the smallest fix. An editor decides every finding.

- **B1 Accuracy.** The Chinese says what the English says: the same figures and conversions, the same comparisons and periods, the same direction of change, the same negations, no sentence left out and nothing added.
- **B2 Language.** Wording, fluency, sentence structure, punctuation and quotation marks follow the house style and read as natural written Chinese.
- **B3 Register.** A regulatory disclosure reports in a measured tone. It never promotes, exaggerates or uses colloquial words.
- **B4 Protected passage.** The sponsor statement (F4) is the sponsor's wording. Only accuracy corrections are made there (a part missing, wrong or added against the English); its wording and tone are never changed.

Terms the glossary settles are already corrected by the glossary stage before the review; do not report them again.
