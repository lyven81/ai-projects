# Clinic Hours and Rota

Sang Kancil Specialist Clinic (fictional demo clinic, Kota Damansara, Selangor). Maintained resource read by check_schedule and book_appointment (OP-03). Valid from 2026-10-01 to 2026-12-31; a date outside it is reported as a gap and referred to Nurul Huda. Times are Malaysia time. Prepared 22 October 2026 from the design discussion; a demo rota, not an operating clinic's roster.
