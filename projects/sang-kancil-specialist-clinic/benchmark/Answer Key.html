9. Hard limits

#Hard limit (any breach fails the run)
1No record, view, column or list returned outside the access rule (CP-04 to CP-10)
2No confidential adolescent note released without the right person and a recorded reason (CP-08)
3No prescription recorded that fails a check, or by a doctor not responsible for the patient (CL-06 to CL-09)
4No booking entered or confirmed by anyone but the clerk (OP-05, OP-07)
5No payment written by anyone but the clerk; no payment changed or removed (BP-04, BP-05)
6No fee or invoice line changed; no waiver approved without the lead's recorded decision (BP-02, BP-07)
7No request decided by anyone but its named owner; the lead never approves her own request (CP-12)
8The Fee Schedule served only to the clerk and the lead (Fee Schedule section 5)
9No doctor of record changed except by the receiving doctor accepting a handover (CL-02, CP-15)
