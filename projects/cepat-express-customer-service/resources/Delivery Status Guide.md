# Delivery Status Guide, Cepat Express

Owner: Customer Service, with Operations. 2 Nov 2026. What each shipment status means and what the agent advises (Customer Service Policy S1, S4, S7). The status comes from the status tool by tracking number; this guide never holds a parcel's record. Limits are the Courier Policy's (C-numbers).

| Status | ID | What it means | Limit that applies | What the agent advises |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Booked | D1 | The sender has booked the parcel; it is not yet picked up, so not yet shipped (C2). | None (C5 counts only from pickup) | Politely tells the customer that, by the record, the sender has not yet sent the parcel to Cepat Express, so delivery starts once it is picked up. No transfer. An address change can still be requested, and goes to the Logistics Officer (C11, S14). A customer asking for the pickup, after the phone match: say where the parcel is going, and the request goes to the Logistics Officer of the pickup location (C18, S20c). |
| Picked up | D2 | The rider has collected the parcel from the sender. | C4: delivery in 2 working days from pickup | The parcel is on its way, with the expected delivery date. |
| At sorting hub | D3 | The parcel is at a sorting hub (Ipoh, Johor Bahru or Klang). | C6: 48 hours at a hub | Within 48 hours: where it is and that it moves on shortly, with the expected date. Past 48 hours: the latest status, then that the delay has been flagged for customer service to follow up with the team at that hub (S20; code routes it to the Logistics Officer there). |
| In transit | D4 | The parcel is moving between hubs or to the delivery location. | C5: 3 days with no new scan | Within 3 days: the last scan and the expected date. Over 3 days: the latest status, then that the delay has been flagged for customer service to follow up with the team at the last-scan location (S20). Over 7 days: lost (C15, S12). |
| Out for delivery | D5 | A rider has the parcel for delivery today. | C7: delivered by 9 pm | Before 9 pm: it is on its way today. After 9 pm with no delivery scan: the latest status, then that the missed delivery has been flagged for customer service to follow up with the team at the delivery location (S20). |
| Delivery attempted | D6 | The rider tried to deliver and no one received it; the scan carries the reason (C8). | C9: one reattempt, then 5 working days at the branch | First attempt: the reattempt date. After the second: the parcel is at the branch until the last collection day; self-collection goes to the Logistics Officer (S20b). |
| Delivered | D7 | The parcel was handed over, with proof of delivery (C10). | None | Delivered, with the date and time. The proof of delivery photo needs the phone match (S4). A customer who did not receive it is handed over (S16). |
| Rejected by recipient | D8 | The recipient refused the parcel. | None | The parcel was rejected and the case goes to Customer Service for the return (S13). |

**Any status, a parcel reported lost:** with a scan within 7 days, the agent politely advises that the parcel is not missing and is on its way, with its last scan (C15).

**Unknown tracking number:** the agent says no parcel matches that number and asks the customer to check it. It never guesses a near match.
