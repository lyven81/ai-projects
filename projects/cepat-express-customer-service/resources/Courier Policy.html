Cepat Express Courier Policy

Cepat Express is a courier serving Ipoh, Johor Bahru and Klang. What a parcel status means and what Cepat Express does about it. The AI agent advises from these clauses; the limits (C4 to C7, C15) are computed in code from the parcel record. A clause under a reply on the demo page opens its row here, highlighted.

The other pages: Customer Service Policy · Delivery Status Guide · Staff Directory · Answer Key

Owner: Operations. Effective 2 Nov 2026.

The Courier Policy says what a parcel status means and what Cepat Express does about it. The Customer Service Policy says what the AI agent and the CS staff do; it cites these clause IDs and never restates them.

1. Parcel status

RulePolicyEnforced by
C1A parcel has one status at a time: Booked (not yet shipped), Picked up, At sorting hub, In transit, Out for delivery, Delivery attempted, Delivered, Rejected by recipient.Code (data)
C2A parcel is shipped from the moment it is Picked up. Before that it is Booked.Code
C3Every status change is a scan, with date, time and location. The last scan is the current status.Code (data)
C3aCepat Express serves three locations: Ipoh, Johor Bahru and Klang, each with a sorting hub and a Logistics Officer. Every parcel's delivery location and every scan location is one of the three.Code (data check)

2. Delivery standards and limits

RulePolicyEnforced by
C4Delivery standard: 2 working days throughout Peninsular Malaysia, counted from pickup.Code (date shown by the tool)
C5No new scan for more than 3 days counts as delayed.Code
C6A parcel at a sorting hub for more than 48 hours counts as delayed.Code
C7A parcel Out for delivery is delivered by 9 pm the same day. After 9 pm with no delivery scan, the delivery has failed.Code

3. Delivery attempts

RulePolicyEnforced by
C8When no one receives the parcel, the rider records a Delivery attempted scan with the reason.Code (data)
C9A missed delivery is attempted again on the next working day, once. After a second failed attempt the parcel waits at the branch for 5 working days for self-collection, then returns to the sender.Prompt (information)
C10A Delivered scan carries proof of delivery: time, location and photo or signature.Code (data)

4. Changes after booking

RulePolicyEnforced by
C11The delivery address can be changed only while the parcel is Booked. The change is made by Logistics.Code
C12Once a parcel is shipped (C2), the address cannot be changed.Code
C13A change of delivery date for a shipped parcel is arranged by Logistics.Prompt (routing in S-policy)

5. Problems with a parcel

RulePolicyEnforced by
C14Damaged parcel, items missing, lost parcel and rejected parcel are registered as a ticket of type damaged, lost or rejected. Items missing is registered as damaged.Code
C15Days with no new scan decide the outcome, one rule: 0 to 3 days, on its way (C5); over 3 to 7 days, delayed (C5); over 7 days, lost. When a customer reports a parcel lost but the tracking record shows a scan within 7 days, the agent politely advises that the parcel is not missing and is on its way, with its last scan.Code
C16Compensation is decided by Customer Service after investigation. No compensation is promised before that.Prompt
C17Delivered but not received, a wrong parcel, a missed delivery while the customer was home, and rider conduct are investigated by the Logistics Officer of the location where the issue happened.Code (routing in S-policy, S20a)

6. Outside customer service

RulePolicyEnforced by
C18A new pickup is booked by the sender through the Cepat Express app or website. A parcel already booked but not yet picked up (Booked, C2) has its pickup arranged by the Logistics Officer of the pickup location, the parcel's origin (S20c).Prompt + Code (routing in S-policy)
C19A special delivery instruction (guardhouse, neighbour) for a parcel not yet delivered is passed to the Logistics Officer of the delivery location, who passes it to the rider (S20d).Prompt + Code (routing in S-policy)
