Cepat Express Delivery Status Guide

Cepat Express is a courier serving Ipoh, Johor Bahru and Klang. What each shipment status means, the limit that applies and what the agent advises. The status itself always comes from the parcel record by tracking number. A clause under a reply on the demo page opens its row here, highlighted.

The other pages: Courier Policy · Customer Service Policy · Staff Directory · Answer Key

Owner: Customer Service, with Operations. 2 Nov 2026. What each shipment status means and what the agent advises (Customer Service Policy S1, S4, S7). The status comes from the status tool by tracking number; this guide never holds a parcel's record. Limits are the Courier Policy's (C-numbers).

StatusIDWhat it meansLimit that appliesWhat the agent advises
BookedD1The sender has booked the parcel; it is not yet picked up, so not yet shipped (C2).None (C5 counts only from pickup)Politely tells the customer that, by the record, the sender has not yet sent the parcel to Cepat Express, so delivery starts once it is picked up. No transfer. An address change can still be requested, and goes to the Logistics Officer (C11, S14). A customer asking for the pickup, after the phone match: say where the parcel is going, and the request goes to the Logistics Officer of the pickup location (C18, S20c).
Picked upD2The rider has collected the parcel from the sender.C4: delivery in 2 working days from pickupThe parcel is on its way, with the expected delivery date.
At sorting hubD3The parcel is at a sorting hub (Ipoh, Johor Bahru or Klang).C6: 48 hours at a hubWithin 48 hours: where it is and that it moves on shortly, with the expected date. Past 48 hours: the latest status, then that the delay has been flagged for customer service to follow up with the team at that hub (S20; code routes it to the Logistics Officer there).
In transitD4The parcel is moving between hubs or to the delivery location.C5: 3 days with no new scanWithin 3 days: the last scan and the expected date. Over 3 days: the latest status, then that the delay has been flagged for customer service to follow up with the team at the last-scan location (S20). Over 7 days: lost (C15, S12).
Out for deliveryD5A rider has the parcel for delivery today.C7: delivered by 9 pmBefore 9 pm: it is on its way today. After 9 pm with no delivery scan: the latest status, then that the missed delivery has been flagged for customer service to follow up with the team at the delivery location (S20).
Delivery attemptedD6The rider tried to deliver and no one received it; the scan carries the reason (C8).C9: one reattempt, then 5 working days at the branchFirst attempt: the reattempt date. After the second: the parcel is at the branch until the last collection day; self-collection goes to the Logistics Officer (S20b).
DeliveredD7The parcel was handed over, with proof of delivery (C10).NoneDelivered, with the date and time. The proof of delivery photo needs the phone match (S4). A customer who did not receive it is handed over (S16).
Rejected by recipientD8The recipient refused the parcel.NoneThe parcel was rejected and the case goes to Customer Service for the return (S13).

Any status, a parcel reported lost: with a scan within 7 days, the agent politely advises that the parcel is not missing and is on its way, with its last scan (C15).

Unknown tracking number: the agent says no parcel matches that number and asks the customer to check it. It never guesses a near match.
