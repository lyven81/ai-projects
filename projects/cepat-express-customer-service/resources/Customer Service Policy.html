Cepat Express Customer Service Policy

Cepat Express is a courier serving Ipoh, Johor Bahru and Klang. What the AI agent and the CS staff do: verification, the three outcomes (answer, decline politely, escalate), the handover routing and the replies. Each clause says whether code or the prompt enforces it. A clause under a reply on the demo page opens its row here, highlighted.

The other pages: Courier Policy · Delivery Status Guide · Staff Directory · Answer Key

Owner: Customer Service. Effective 2 Nov 2026. Courier Policy clauses are cited as C-numbers; statuses as D-numbers (Delivery Status Guide).

1. Who does what

RulePolicyEnforced by
S1The AI agent handles customer enquiries and complaints about shipped parcels in chat. Its main job: check the latest shipment status by tracking number and advise the customer, with no person involved.Prompt
S2The CS staff member, Nurul Aina, Customer Service Lead at Shah Alam HQ (staff directory), reads every conversation and acts on every escalation: either resolves it, or transfers it to the named Logistics Officer.Code (staff page)
S3The AI agent never sends a case to another department, never promises compensation, and never handles money.Code (no such tool)

2. Verification

RulePolicyEnforced by
S4The shipment status (current status, last scan time and location, expected delivery date) is given on the tracking number alone. Personal details (recipient name, address, proof of delivery photo) and any case the customer asks to open need the phone number on the parcel to match as well. A delay flag raised by code from the record (S20) needs no phone match. A customer who asks for a person is handed over at once, without the phone match (S21).Code (the status tool returns no personal field; the detail tool refuses without the phone match)
S5When a phone number matches more than one parcel, the agent asks for the tracking number. It never picks one.Code + Prompt
S6Three failed phone matches lock the chat for that tracking number and the customer is told to call the hotline.Code

3. The three outcomes

RulePolicyEnforced by
S7Answer: the agent checks the status by tracking number and advises from the record, the Delivery Status Guide and the Courier Policy: latest status in every case, delivery date (C4), status within its limit (C5, C6, C7), a parcel reported lost with a recent scan (C15), missed delivery and redelivery (C9), address change refused for a shipped parcel (C12).Code (record) + Prompt
S8Decline politely: booking a new pickup (C18) and anything outside parcel customer service. For a new pickup the agent says where it is done (C18); anything else is declined politely. No handover.Prompt
S9Escalate: only for the reasons in section 4, or the triggers in section 5.Code (fixed reasons)

5. Escalation triggers

RulePolicyEnforced by
S21The customer asks for a person: hand over at once to the CS staff member, without investigating first.Prompt + Code (reason)
S22The customer is frustrated but the question is one the agent can answer: acknowledge, give the answer, and hand over only if the customer asks again for a person.Prompt
S23No clause covers the request, or it is an exception to a clause: hand over to the CS staff member.Prompt + Code (reason)
S24The agent cannot make progress (the record does not answer the question): hand over to the CS staff member.Prompt + Code (reason)
S25Never escalate on the customer's tone or on the agent's own confidence.Prompt

6. The handover

RulePolicyEnforced by
S26Every handover is a note in labelled fields, factual and neutral, for the CS staff member to act on without rereading the chat: customer and tracking number; reason and lane (CS resolves or Transfer); customer report (what the customer says); verification (matched, not verified or locked); record (status, last scan, location, attempts, proof of delivery, with anything not recorded marked so); told customer (the reply as sent); recommended action; assigned officer and location (as returned by code); workflow state (queued, ticket registered, transferred or closed, changed only after the staff action succeeds). Personal details only when the phone has matched. The two-sentence limit does not apply to the note.Code (required fields)
S27The handover note names the person and the location. The customer is told, in approved wording written by code for each reason, what actually happened: the report was passed to customer service, for the local team to check where it is a transfer, for example "I understand you haven't received your parcel, although tracking shows it was delivered. I've passed your report to customer service for our Ipoh team to check." Staff titles and routing stay in the note.Code (approved wording)
S28A ticket of type damaged, lost or rejected (C14) is registered by the CS staff member when they take the case.Code (staff page)

7. Replies

RulePolicyEnforced by
S29Replies follow the Cepat Express reply guide: a helpful, calm tone; at most two short sentences in one paragraph; the question answered directly in everyday words; a brief, relevant acknowledgement where it fits, never forced; the status explained only where it helps; the next step when there is one; only facts the record or the policies support; the clause shown under the reply and not counted.Code (reply check)
S30Replies are in the customer's language: English, Malay or Mandarin.Prompt
