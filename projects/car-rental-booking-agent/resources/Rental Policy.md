# Rental Policy

Kereta Sewa Jalan-jalan (fictional demo car rental company at KLIA). Rates are in inventory://cars. Cite clauses by ID, e.g. R12.

## 1. Hours

- **R1** Bookings, pickup and return are handled from 9am to 6pm, seven days a week. *(Enforced by: Code)*
- **R2** A pickup or return outside 9am to 6pm is passed to staff to arrange. *(Enforced by: Code (handover))*
- **R3** A booking request made outside 9am to 6pm is recorded as pending; staff review it from 9am. *(Enforced by: Code)*

## 2. Booking and deposit

- **R4** A booking needs the customer's name, a mobile number (Malaysian or international) and a driving licence number. The licence itself is checked by staff at pickup (R43). *(Enforced by: Code (details present))*
- **R5** Every booking needs a deposit of RM 100, paid when booking. *(Enforced by: Code)*
- **R6** A booking request blocks the car until staff confirm or reject it. A car blocked or rented for any part of the requested period cannot be booked. *(Enforced by: Code)*
- **R7** The customer reports the deposit payment by uploading the receipt or by saying it was paid. Staff check the bank record; the booking stays pending until staff confirm it. *(Enforced by: Code (no confirm tool))*
- **R8** One mobile number may hold up to 2 car bookings at the same time. More cars are passed to staff. *(Enforced by: Code)*
- **R9** The deposit is released within 48 hours after the car is returned with no damage and a full tank (R18). *(Enforced by: Staff)*

## 3. Pickup, return and delivery

- **R10** Pickup and return at KLIA are free. *(Enforced by: Code)*
- **R11** Pickup or return at any other place costs RM 1 per km from KLIA. The staff member who delivers or collects the car works out the distance. *(Enforced by: Staff)*

## 4. Rental period and price

- **R12** The minimum rental is 2 hours. Up to 5 hours is charged by the hour; 6 hours or more counts as one day at the daily rate. *(Enforced by: Code)*
- **R13** Beyond whole days, extra hours are charged at the hourly rate, capped at one more day. *(Enforced by: Code)*
- **R14** Whole weeks are charged at the weekly rate and remaining days at the daily rate, capped at the two-week rate. *(Enforced by: Code)*
- **R15** A rented car can be driven at any hour, every day of the week, for the whole rental period. *(Enforced by: Prompt)*

## 5. Bookings passed to staff

- **R16** A rental that starts more than 30 days from today, or on the day of the request, is passed to staff. The agent does not block the car. *(Enforced by: Code (handover))*
- **R17** A rental longer than 2 weeks is passed to staff, who may offer a long-term rate. The agent does not quote or promise a discount. *(Enforced by: Code (handover))*

## 6. Fuel

- **R18** Every car is released with a full tank and must be returned with a full tank. Otherwise the cost of refuelling is deducted from the deposit. *(Enforced by: Staff)*

## 7. Cancellation and refund

- **R19** A cancellation 24 hours or more before pickup: the RM 100 deposit is refunded in full. *(Enforced by: Code (quote))*
- **R20** A cancellation less than 24 hours before pickup: the deposit is kept. *(Enforced by: Code (quote))*
- **R21** No-show: the car is not picked up within 2 hours of the pickup time without notice. The booking is cancelled and the deposit kept. *(Enforced by: Staff)*
- **R22** A cancellation is a request until staff confirm it. The agent records it; staff process the refund. *(Enforced by: Code (no cancel tool))*
- **R23** Refunds go back to the original payment method within 24 hours of staff confirming the cancellation. *(Enforced by: Staff)*
- **R24** If the company cancels (car unavailable), the customer chooses a full deposit refund or a car of the same or higher tier at the same price. *(Enforced by: Staff)*
- **R25** Returning early: no refund for unused hours or days. *(Enforced by: Prompt)*
- **R26** A booking request that staff reject because no payment was found is cancelled; nothing is charged or refunded. *(Enforced by: Staff)*
- **R27** A cancellation for an emergency (flight cancelled, medical) less than 24 hours before pickup is decided by staff, with proof. *(Enforced by: Staff)*
- **R28** Changing the dates, time or car of a booking is handled by staff. *(Enforced by: Code (no change tool))*

## 8. Late return

- **R29** A grace period of 30 minutes after the return time, with no charge. *(Enforced by: Staff)*
- **R30** Beyond the grace period, each hour or part hour is charged at the tier's hourly rate. *(Enforced by: Staff)*
- **R31** Late charges for one day are capped at the daily rate; 6 hours or more late is charged as one extra day. *(Enforced by: Staff)*
- **R32** To extend, the customer asks at least 2 hours before the return time; staff confirm, subject to availability. *(Enforced by: Code (handover))*
- **R33** Late charges are deducted from the deposit; any balance is paid at return. *(Enforced by: Staff)*
- **R34** A late return that ends after 6pm needs staff to arrange the return. *(Enforced by: Code (handover))*
- **R35** A car not returned and not contactable 24 hours after the return time is treated as unauthorised use and may be reported to the police. *(Enforced by: Staff)*
- **R36** The car is returned to KLIA. Collection from elsewhere is arranged by staff at RM 1 per km, worked out by the staff member who collects it. *(Enforced by: Staff)*
- **R37** If a late return stops the next customer's booking, the company offers the next customer another car; the late customer pays the late charges only. *(Enforced by: Staff)*
- **R38** The customer tells the company as soon as they know they will be late. *(Enforced by: Code (handover))*

## 9. Driver and vehicle use

- **R39** The driver is 20 to 70 years old. *(Enforced by: Staff (at pickup))*
- **R40** The driver has held a full licence for at least 2 years. Probationary (P) licences are not accepted. *(Enforced by: Staff (at pickup))*
- **R41** A Malaysian class D licence is accepted. *(Enforced by: Staff (at pickup))*
- **R42** A foreign driver needs a valid national licence plus an International Driving Permit, unless the licence is in English. *(Enforced by: Staff (at pickup))*
- **R43** At pickup, the driver shows the licence and IC or passport; the name must match the booking. *(Enforced by: Staff (at pickup))*
- **R44** Only the person who booked may drive. Damage while anyone else drives is charged to the renter in full. *(Enforced by: Staff)*
- **R45** No smoking in the car. A cleaning fee is deducted from the deposit if the car smells of smoke. *(Enforced by: Staff)*
- **R46** No pets, unless agreed with staff in advance. *(Enforced by: Staff)*
- **R47** The car may not be driven out of Peninsular Malaysia (no Singapore or Thailand) without staff approval. *(Enforced by: Code (handover))*
- **R48** Traffic summonses and tolls during the rental are paid by the renter. *(Enforced by: Prompt)*

## 10. How the agent uses this policy

- Covered by policy: answer from this policy and cite the clause.
- Contradicts policy: decline, state the rule, offer what the rule does allow. No exceptions in chat.
- Not covered by policy: do not guess. Hand to staff (reason not_in_policy).
- Policy neither says yes nor no: do not choose a reading. Hand to staff (reason not_in_policy), naming the clause that seemed to apply and why it is unclear.
