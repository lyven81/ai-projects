3. How a rental is priced

RuleHow
R12Minimum 2 hours. Up to 5 hours, hours times the hourly rate. 6 hours or more counts as one day.
R13Beyond whole days, extra hours at the hourly rate, capped at one more day. Standard, 1 day and 3 hours: RM 120 + RM 60 = RM 180.
R14Whole weeks at the weekly rate, remaining days at the daily rate, capped at the two-week rate. Standard, 9 days: RM 700 + 2 x RM 120 = RM 940.
R5Every booking also needs the RM 100 deposit.
