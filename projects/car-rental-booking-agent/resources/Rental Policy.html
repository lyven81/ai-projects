Rental Policy

Kereta Sewa Jalan-jalan is a fictional car rental company at KLIA used for demonstration. This policy is an MCP resource: the AI agent reads it to answer customers, and the booking tools enforce the rules marked Code. Rates are in the Car Inventory.

Enforced by: Code means a tool refuses or calculates it; Staff means a person decides; Prompt means the agent states it. Anything not written here goes to staff.

1. Hours

RulePolicyEnforced by
R1Bookings, pickup and return are handled from 9am to 6pm, seven days a week.Code
R2A pickup or return outside 9am to 6pm is passed to staff to arrange.Code (handover)
R3A booking request made outside 9am to 6pm is recorded as pending; staff review it from 9am.Code

2. Booking and deposit

RulePolicyEnforced by
R4A booking needs the customer's name, a mobile number (Malaysian or international) and a driving licence number. The licence itself is checked by staff at pickup (R43).Code (details present)
R5Every booking needs a deposit of RM 100, paid when booking.Code
R6A booking request blocks the car until staff confirm or reject it. A car blocked or rented for any part of the requested period cannot be booked.Code
R7The customer reports the deposit payment by uploading the receipt or by saying it was paid. Staff check the bank record; the booking stays pending until staff confirm it.Code (no confirm tool)
R8One mobile number may hold up to 2 car bookings at the same time. More cars are passed to staff.Code
R9The deposit is released within 48 hours after the car is returned with no damage and a full tank (R18).Staff

3. Pickup, return and delivery

RulePolicyEnforced by
R10Pickup and return at KLIA are free.Code
R11Pickup or return at any other place costs RM 1 per km from KLIA. The staff member who delivers or collects the car works out the distance.Staff

4. Rental period and price

RulePolicyEnforced by
R12The minimum rental is 2 hours. Up to 5 hours is charged by the hour; 6 hours or more counts as one day at the daily rate.Code
R13Beyond whole days, extra hours are charged at the hourly rate, capped at one more day.Code
R14Whole weeks are charged at the weekly rate and remaining days at the daily rate, capped at the two-week rate.Code
R15A rented car can be driven at any hour, every day of the week, for the whole rental period.Prompt

5. Bookings passed to staff

RulePolicyEnforced by
R16A rental that starts more than 30 days from today, or on the day of the request, is passed to staff. The agent does not block the car.Code (handover)
R17A rental longer than 2 weeks is passed to staff, who may offer a long-term rate. The agent does not quote or promise a discount.Code (handover)

6. Fuel

RulePolicyEnforced by
R18Every car is released with a full tank and must be returned with a full tank. Otherwise the cost of refuelling is deducted from the deposit.Staff

7. Cancellation and refund

RulePolicyEnforced by
R19A cancellation 24 hours or more before pickup: the RM 100 deposit is refunded in full.Code (quote)
R20A cancellation less than 24 hours before pickup: the deposit is kept.Code (quote)
R21No-show: the car is not picked up within 2 hours of the pickup time without notice. The booking is cancelled and the deposit kept.Staff
R22A cancellation is a request until staff confirm it. The agent records it; staff process the refund.Code (no cancel tool)
R23Refunds go back to the original payment method within 24 hours of staff confirming the cancellation.Staff
R24If the company cancels (car unavailable), the customer chooses a full deposit refund or a car of the same or higher tier at the same price.Staff
R25Returning early: no refund for unused hours or days.Prompt
R26A booking request that staff reject because no payment was found is cancelled; nothing is charged or refunded.Staff
R27A cancellation for an emergency (flight cancelled, medical) less than 24 hours before pickup is decided by staff, with proof.Staff
R28Changing the dates, time or car of a booking is handled by staff.Code (no change tool)

8. Late return

RulePolicyEnforced by
R29A grace period of 30 minutes after the return time, with no charge.Staff
R30Beyond the grace period, each hour or part hour is charged at the tier's hourly rate.Staff
R31Late charges for one day are capped at the daily rate; 6 hours or more late is charged as one extra day.Staff
R32To extend, the customer asks at least 2 hours before the return time; staff confirm, subject to availability.Code (handover)
R33Late charges are deducted from the deposit; any balance is paid at return.Staff
R34A late return that ends after 6pm needs staff to arrange the return.Code (handover)
R35A car not returned and not contactable 24 hours after the return time is treated as unauthorised use and may be reported to the police.Staff
R36The car is returned to KLIA. Collection from elsewhere is arranged by staff at RM 1 per km, worked out by the staff member who collects it.Staff
R37If a late return stops the next customer's booking, the company offers the next customer another car; the late customer pays the late charges only.Staff
R38The customer tells the company as soon as they know they will be late.Code (handover)

9. Driver and vehicle use

RulePolicyEnforced by
R39The driver is 20 to 70 years old.Staff (at pickup)
R40The driver has held a full licence for at least 2 years. Probationary (P) licences are not accepted.Staff (at pickup)
R41A Malaysian class D licence is accepted.Staff (at pickup)
R42A foreign driver needs a valid national licence plus an International Driving Permit, unless the licence is in English.Staff (at pickup)
R43At pickup, the driver shows the licence and IC or passport; the name must match the booking.Staff (at pickup)
R44Only the person who booked may drive. Damage while anyone else drives is charged to the renter in full.Staff
R45No smoking in the car. A cleaning fee is deducted from the deposit if the car smells of smoke.Staff
R46No pets, unless agreed with staff in advance.Staff
R47The car may not be driven out of Peninsular Malaysia (no Singapore or Thailand) without staff approval.Code (handover)
R48Traffic summonses and tolls during the rental are paid by the renter.Prompt

10. How the agent uses this policy

EnquiryWhat the agent does
Covered by policyAnswers from this policy and names the rule (e.g. R12).
Contradicts policyDeclines, states the rule that applies, and offers what the rule does allow. No exceptions are made in chat.
Not covered by policyDoes not guess. Hands the enquiry to staff with the structured handover.
Policy neither says yes nor noDoes not choose a reading. Hands the enquiry to staff, naming the rule that seemed to apply and why it is unclear.
