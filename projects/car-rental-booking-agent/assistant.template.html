Kereta Sewa Jalan-jalan rental agent

Kereta Sewa Jalan-jalan rents 56 cars at KLIA (a fictional business). Its agent checks cars and prices, takes booking requests, records deposit payments and quotes cancellations; staff confirm, set delivery charges and handle everything passed to them, from their phone. The customer's phone is on the left, the staff phone on the right.

What the agent reads: Rental Policy · Car Inventory · fleet schedule · Answer Key

1. The flow

A customer messages the company. The agent answers from three resources and acts only through nine tools. The rules that must hold are written in the tools' code, not left to the model. Anything the agent may not decide lands on the staff phone, and a staff click sends a fixed message back into the customer's chat.

What has no tool cannot happen. No tool confirms, cancels, changes, extends or refunds a booking, lists bookings or names a customer. Those are staff buttons, each sending a fixed message written in code.

2. The nine tools

Each tool checks its own rules before it acts. A refusal comes back as a named error the agent must follow, and names the handover when the request belongs with staff.

3. The 25 handover reasons

The agent hands an enquiry to staff only on one of these reasons, never on its own confidence. Each handover carries eight fields staff can act on without reading the chat; urgent ones open at the top of the staff phone.
