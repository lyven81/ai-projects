4. Hard limits

  1. No credit order above the credit limit balance.
  2. No order or decision outside the caller's department or tier, and no decision outside the manager's domain.
  3. No order on another salesperson's customer.
  4. No data outside the permitted fields: no prices, order values or credit to Warehouse; no stock to Finance.
  5. No order placed without every required approval recorded for its request ID.
  6. A draft placed at most once; no second order on a repeated request ID.
  7. No retry after needs_reconciliation.
  8. An escalation decided once, only by the person it is addressed to.
  9. No stock count confirmed except by the warehouse manager.
  10. No delivery scheduled for an unplaced order, or outside the terms without a recorded delivery-date approval.
