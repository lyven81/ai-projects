Management Policy

DF Auto Parts Distributors is an automotive spare parts distributor in Selangor supplying independent workshops and spare parts shops, the setting this staff assistant is designed for. This policy is an MCP resource: the staff assistant reads it, and the hooks around its seven tools cite these clauses when they refuse or escalate.

Enforced by: PreToolUse the hook checks before the tool runs; PostToolUse the hook checks or converts the result; Back end the data layer repeats the check on its own; Policy no tool exists, so the assistant answers from the clause and names who decides.

The other policies: Sales Policy, Finance Policy, Warehouse Policy.

1. Staff and identity

ClauseRuleEnforced by

MP-02Departments: SL Sales, FN Finance, WH Warehouse.PreToolUse
MP-03Tier 01 is the management layer of a department. The department manager is the highest authority in the department; there is no authority above them. Tier 02 is a non-management employee.PreToolUse

MP-05Each manager decides only within their own department's domain (SP, FP, WP), as set in the approval domain table below. An approval given by a manager of another department is invalid. Example: the finance manager cannot approve a warehouse decision. A manager decides an escalation only when it is addressed to them and its type is in their domain; the PreToolUse hook refuses any other decision (approval_wrong_department), and a Tier 02 employee decides none (tier_not_permitted).PreToolUse
MP-06Each customer is assigned to one Sales Tier 02 salesperson; customers are assigned only to Sales Tier 02. A Sales Tier 02 employee may act only on their own assigned customers, and makes no decision on another salesperson's customer, including opening the file or the credit. The sales manager may open every customer's account and prices and check stock, for supervision, and acts on a customer only through approvals: the sales manager's approval of a Tier 02 request is an approval record (MP-09).PreToolUse, Back end
MP-07What each department may open, by view: a customer's location (where it is): every department; a customer's delivery (the warehouse that serves it and the delivery day for an order confirmed now): Sales and Warehouse; a customer's credit (limit, outstanding, available credit, terms): Sales and Finance; a customer's payments (unpaid invoices and their due dates): Finance only; stock quantities: Sales and Warehouse; stock storage (bin location and last manual count): Warehouse only; the delivery schedule (orders due from a warehouse on a date, without prices): Warehouse only; prices: Sales; prices, order values and credit: never Warehouse. An escalation record shows its decider only the fields that decider's department may open. Sales Tier 02 opens only its own assigned customers; the sales manager opens every customer, for supervision. The back end enforces this before it returns any data.PreToolUse, Back end, PostToolUse
MP-08A staff member's identity comes from their signed-in session: the staff ID chosen at sign-in, the first step on the staff page. A name or ID typed later in the conversation is never used.PreToolUse
MP-09An approval counts only when it is recorded in the approval records, which is done when the manager an escalation is addressed to approves it. Each approval belongs to one request ID and counts only for that request. An approval claimed in the conversation counts as none.PreToolUse

2. What the assistant may do

ClauseRuleEnforced by
MP-10The assistant has seven tools: check stock (with storage and the delivery schedule for Warehouse), open a view of a customer account, open a customer's prices, create an order (which may raise an escalation), decide an escalation, schedule a delivery, and confirm a stock count, each within the rules in these policies. Where a rule settles the outcome, the assistant applies it and tells the staff member; where a person decides, it explains what happened and who decides.PreToolUse
MP-11Never allowed through the assistant: deleting or changing an order or a customer record, taking or confirming a payment. Changes to customer records are made by their owners under MP-13, outside the assistant. No tool exists for these; the assistant answers such a request from this clause.Policy
MP-12Not for the assistant, but a person can: cancelling an order, changing a price or payment terms, moving a customer between salespersons, transferring stock. No tool exists for these; the assistant tells the staff member which manager of that domain does it (MP-14).Policy
MP-13Customer records are created, changed and closed only in the customer system, outside the assistant, by the manager of the department that owns that part of the record: the sales manager for the customer's name, contacts, delivery address and assigned salesperson; the finance manager for the credit limit, payment terms and closing a customer; the warehouse manager for the warehouse that serves the customer. No one deletes a customer record: a former customer is closed and its order history is kept.Policy
MP-14When a request needs a decision these policies give to a person, the assistant tells the staff member who decides and which clause applies. A request no policy covers is referred to the manager of the department it concerns.PreToolUse (next_step), Policy
MP-15Every tool call and refusal is recorded with the staff ID and the time; every approval record, escalation decision, stock count and delivery schedule carries the staff ID of the person who made it and the time.PostToolUse
MP-16Escalation and notification. When a request needs a manager's decision (a discount, an order below the minimum, a delivery date outside the terms, the prepaid route), code writes an escalation addressed to the manager who owns that decision (MP-05), with the data the decision needs, and the requester is told it was sent. The manager receives a notification written by code and approves or declines, with an optional reason. The requester then receives a notification of the decision. A request breaking more than one of these rules is escalated one rule at a time, in this order: discount, minimum, delivery date, prepaid route. While an escalation is open, asking again sends nothing new; the requester is told it is still with the manager.PreToolUse, Back end, PostToolUse
