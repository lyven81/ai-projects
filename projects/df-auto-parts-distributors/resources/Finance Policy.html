Finance Policy

DF Auto Parts Distributors is an automotive spare parts distributor in Selangor supplying independent workshops and spare parts shops, the setting this staff assistant is designed for. This policy is an MCP resource: the staff assistant reads it, and the hooks around its seven tools cite these clauses when they refuse or escalate.

Enforced by: PreToolUse the hook checks before the tool runs; PostToolUse the hook checks or converts the result; Back end the data layer repeats the check on its own; Policy no tool exists, so the assistant answers from the clause and names who decides.

The other policies: Management Policy, Sales Policy, Warehouse Policy.

1. Credit

ClauseRuleEnforced by
FP-01Each customer has a credit limit, a credit limit balance (the credit still available) and an outstanding balance. Credit limit balance = credit limit minus outstanding balance. It can be negative when the outstanding balance is above the limit.PostToolUse
FP-02Credit is visible to Finance for every customer, to the assigned salesperson for their own customers, and to the sales manager for every customer. Warehouse does not see credit.Back end, PostToolUse
FP-03Credit route: an order on credit always requires a credit limit balance at least equal to the order total. No approval from any department overrides this.PreToolUse, Back end
FP-04Prepaid route: with the finance manager's recorded approval, an order is created as a separate prepaid order. It uses no credit, is held as awaiting payment, and is not dispatched until Finance confirms payment outside the assistant. A prepaid approval never turns a credit order into an allowed one.PreToolUse
FP-05When a credit order exceeds the balance, the options are: a smaller order, the prepaid route (FP-04), or a credit raise by Finance (FP-06 to FP-08).PreToolUse (next_step)

2. Credit raises

ClauseRuleEnforced by
FP-06A credit raise increases the customer's credit limit; the credit limit balance is then recalculated (FP-01). Credit raises are made by Finance in its own system, outside the assistant. Finance Tier 02 may raise credit only when the current balance is above zero and the balance after the raise is at most RM 2,000. Example: a balance of RM 1,900 allows a Tier 02 raise of RM 100 at most. Any other raise by Finance Tier 02 is for the finance manager.Policy
FP-07The finance manager may approve any credit raise. A raise that takes the balance beyond RM 2,000, or any raise when the current balance is zero or negative, is decided by the finance manager only.Policy
FP-08No one outside Finance approves a credit raise, the sales manager included.Policy

3. Terms and payments

ClauseRuleEnforced by
FP-09Payment terms are set per customer (30 days by default) and changed only by the finance manager, outside the assistant.Policy
FP-10The assistant never takes, records or confirms a payment.Policy
