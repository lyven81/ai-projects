Maintenance charge and late interest		My bill shows charges of RM 200 and a sinking fund of RM 12. My friend says the sinking fund must be at least 10%. Which is right? I was also charged late interest.Tools: search_rules, submit_guidance
Must cite: ACT-s61; ABL-8; BL-6 or ACT-s60
Handover: none
Facts still missing must be named
Conflict: ACT-s61 governs		The conflict case: ABL-8 sets the sinking fund at 6%, but Act s.61(3) says not less than 10% of the charges. The Act governs. Late interest of 10% a year is within s.60(3)(c) and by-law 6(2). No figure is calculated. Interest under by-law 6(2) runs only 14 days after a notice, and the notice date is not given, so that fact is missing. RM 12 is below the Act's minimum; the reply implies no refund.passedpassed
