59. Within five days from the date of completion of inspection of the affected parcel, any other parcel or the common property or limited common property or within such extended time as the Commissioner may grant, the developer, joint management body, management corporation or subsidiary management corporation or any managing agent appointed by the Commissioner under subsection 86(1) or 91(3) of the Act, as the case may be, shall issue a certificate of inspection in Form 2 8 to state the cause of the inter-floor leakage and the party responsible to rectify it.
