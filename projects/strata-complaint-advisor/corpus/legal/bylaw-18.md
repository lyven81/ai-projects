(4) A proprietor shall not remove or tamper with any fire fighting installation and equipment installed in the building or the common property.

(5) A proprietor shall not do anything in his parcel or on the common property that is likely to—

(a) affect the operation of any safety installation, equipment or devices, or reduce the level of fire safety in the building or the common property; or

(b) create a hazard or danger to any other proprietor in the building or any person lawfully using the common property.

(3) A proprietor shall not leave unattended any stove, fire or heating appliance that may cause a fire to the building due to overheating of the stove or heating appliance.
