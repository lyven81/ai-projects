## HR-1. Quiet hours
(1) Quiet hours are from 11.00 p.m. to 7.00 a.m. every day. Music, television, karaoke, musical instruments and other sound in a parcel or on the common property shall be kept at a low volume during quiet hours, as required by by-law 10(3) of the Third Schedule.
(2) A resident disturbed by noise may report it to the guardhouse at any hour or to the management office. A report should give the time, the duration, the unit believed to be the source, and whether it has happened before.
(3) The guard on duty may attend and ask that the volume be lowered. The source of the noise is recorded as reported until staff have verified it.

## HR-2. Bulky waste
(1) Furniture, mattresses, appliances and other bulky items shall not be left at the refuse chamber, the refuse chute or any common area, as provided in by-law 26(5) of the Third Schedule.
(2) Bulky waste collection is every Wednesday. A resident shall book a collection slot with the management office by Tuesday at 5.00 p.m. and bring the items to the loading bay of his block between 9.00 a.m. and 11.00 a.m. on the Wednesday.
(3) Items left at a refuse chamber are recorded by the management office and the resident concerned, once identified, is asked to remove them or book a collection.

## HR-3. Swimming pool and facilities
(1) The swimming pool, gymnasium and function room are open from 7.00 a.m. to 10.00 p.m.
(2) Each parcel may bring up to 4 guests to the swimming pool at one time. Guests shall be accompanied by a resident of that parcel throughout their visit and registered at the guardhouse.
(3) Pool users shall wear proper swimming attire, shower before entering the pool, and follow the instructions of the lifeguard or staff on duty.

## HR-4. Visitor registration
(1) Every visitor shall register at the guardhouse before entering, giving his name, the parcel visited and the vehicle registration number if any, and showing an identification document as required under by-law 17 of the Third Schedule.
(2) The guard records only the last four digits of an identification number. The document is returned to the visitor at once and is not kept.
(3) A visitor who declines to identify himself may be refused entry. The guard shall call the resident of the parcel visited, who may meet the visitor at the guardhouse.
(4) Residents may pre-register expected visitors through the management office.

## HR-5. Smoking and cooking odours
(1) Smoking is not permitted on any common property, including lifts, lift lobbies, corridors, staircases, the car park and the facilities.
(2) Residents smoking on their own balconies shall take reasonable care that smoke does not enter neighbouring parcels, and shall not throw cigarette butts from any window or balcony.
(3) Burning of incense, joss paper or any other material is permitted only at the designated burning point on the ground floor of each block. Residents shall use cooker hoods and take reasonable care that strong cooking odours do not spread into the common corridors.

## HR-6. Reporting faults in common property
(1) Faults in common property, including blocked or broken drains, lights, lifts and doors, shall be reported to the management office, which logs each report as a maintenance work order and gives the reporter its reference number.
(2) Urgent faults affecting safety shall be reported to the guardhouse at once.

## HR-7. Proposed amendment: wheel clamp release charge (draft, not approved)
(1) The wheel clamp release charge shall be increased to RM 300.
(2) This proposal was tabled at the management committee meeting of 10 May 2025 and was not adopted. It has no effect.
