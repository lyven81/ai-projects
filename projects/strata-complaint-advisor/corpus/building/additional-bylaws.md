## ABL-1. Application
(1) These additional by-laws are made by the Management Corporation of Amber Residences by special resolution under section 70(2) of the Strata Management Act 2013, and apply to Blocks A, B, C and D and the common property of the development.
(2) They add to the by-laws in the Third Schedule of the Strata Management (Maintenance and Management) Regulations 2015. Where any of them is inconsistent with the Act or the Third Schedule, the Act or the Third Schedule prevails.

## ABL-2. Parking and wheel clamping
(1) Each parcel is allocated the parking bay or bays shown in the car park register kept at the management office. A vehicle shall be parked only in the bay allocated to its parcel or in a visitor bay.
(2) A vehicle parked in a bay allocated to another parcel, outside a marked bay, or in a visitor bay for more than 24 hours may be wheel-clamped under by-law 25 of the Third Schedule.
(3) The charge for the release of a wheel clamp is RM 100, payable at the management office, together with any towing cost and holding charge actually incurred.
(4) A resident whose allocated bay is occupied shall report the vehicle registration number, the bay number and the time to the management office or the guardhouse. Staff shall verify the report on site before any clamp is applied.

## ABL-3. Pets
(1) A resident keeping a cat or a dog shall register it with the management office within 14 days, giving the parcel number and a description of the animal.
(2) A dog on the common property shall be kept on a leash and under control at all times. Pets are not permitted in the swimming pool area, the gymnasium or the children's playground.
(3) A resident shall immediately clean up any fouling by his pet on the common property.
(4) Barking, howling or other noise from a pet that persists so as to disturb other residents is an annoyance or nuisance within the meaning of by-law 14(1) of the Third Schedule, and the management may give the written notice referred to in by-law 14(2).

## ABL-4. Renovation hours and noisy works
(1) Renovation works approved under by-law 27 of the Third Schedule may be carried out only between 9.00 a.m. and 5.30 p.m. from Monday to Friday, and between 9.00 a.m. and 1.00 p.m. on Saturday. No renovation works are permitted on Sundays or public holidays.
(2) Hacking, drilling, grinding and any other works producing loud noise or vibration may be carried out only between 10.00 a.m. and 4.00 p.m. from Monday to Friday.
(3) Works outside these hours may be stopped by the management office, and the proprietor remains responsible for his contractor under by-law 27(10) of the Third Schedule.

## ABL-5. Renovation deposit and protection of common property
(1) A proprietor applying for approval of renovation works shall place a renovation deposit of RM 1,000 with the management office before works begin.
(2) The proprietor shall ensure his contractor installs the protective padding provided by the management office in the service lift before moving materials, and uses only the service lift.
(3) Damage to the common property, including the lifts, caused by the renovation works shall be assessed by the management office. The cost of making good the damage shall be deducted from the renovation deposit, and any cost above the deposit is recoverable from the proprietor under by-law 27(7) of the Third Schedule.
(4) The balance of the deposit shall be released 3 days after the scheduled completion of the works, after a joint inspection of the common property.

## ABL-6. Moving in and moving out
(1) A resident moving in or out shall give the management office at least 3 working days' notice of the scheduled date and time, and place a moving deposit of RM 300.
(2) Moving is permitted between 9.00 a.m. and 6.00 p.m. from Monday to Saturday, using the service lift with protective padding. The management office may decline a date already booked by another resident or falling on a Sunday or public holiday, and shall offer the nearest available date.
(3) The moving deposit shall be released 3 days after the scheduled move, after a joint inspection of the lift and the common areas used. The cost of any damage found shall be deducted from the deposit.

## ABL-7. Belongings in common corridors
(1) No shoes, shoe racks, bicycles, potted plants, furniture or other belongings shall be left in a common corridor, staircase or lift lobby, as provided in by-laws 20 and 22(4) of the Third Schedule.
(2) Items removed by the management under by-law 20(2) of the Third Schedule may be claimed within 14 days from the date of the notice on payment of a reclaim charge of RM 150.

## ABL-8. Maintenance charges, sinking fund and late payment
(1) The charges payable for the maintenance account are RM 200 a month for each parcel, as determined at the annual general meeting of 15 March 2025, all parcels carrying equal share units.
(2) The contribution to the sinking fund is 6% of the charges.
(3) Charges and sinking fund contributions are payable monthly in advance, whether the parcel is occupied or vacant.
(4) Interest on late payment is charged at 10% a year on a daily basis, as provided in by-law 6(2) of the Third Schedule.

## ABL-9. Fines
(1) Under by-law 7 of the Third Schedule and section 70(2)(i) of the Act, the general meeting of 15 March 2025 resolved that a fine of RM 50 may be imposed for each breach of these additional by-laws or the Third Schedule by-laws for which no other fine is set. The wheel clamp release charge, the reclaim charge and the access card reactivation charge are charges, not fines.
(2) A fine is imposed only by the management corporation after the breach has been verified and the person concerned has been notified in writing. Staff of the management office do not impose fines on their own.
