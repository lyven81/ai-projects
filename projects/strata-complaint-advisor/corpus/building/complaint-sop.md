# Complaint handling SOP, management office of Amber Residences

The admin officer handles complaints from residents of Blocks A to D. A complaint is a report by a resident. It is not a finding that anyone has broken a rule until staff have verified the facts.

## The steps

## SOP-1. Acknowledge
Acknowledge every complaint within one working day, by the channel the resident used.

## SOP-2. Collect the facts
Record what was reported, when, where (unit or area), who is believed to be involved, and what evidence exists. Ask the resident for what is missing before any action against another resident.

## SOP-3. Check the rules
Find the rule that applies in the Strata Management Act 2013, the 2015 Regulations and the Third Schedule by-laws, the building's additional by-laws and the house rules. Where two documents differ, the Act governs, then the Regulations and the Third Schedule, then the additional by-laws, then the house rules.

## SOP-4. Verify on site
Where a complaint concerns a condition or another unit, a staff member or the guard on duty checks it on site and records what was seen, with the date and time.

## SOP-5. Written notice
Once a breach has been verified, the management office may send a written notice or reminder to the parcel concerned, citing the rule and the step required. A notice is never sent on an unverified report.

## SOP-6. Work order
A fault in the common property is logged as a maintenance work order, and the resident is given the reference number.

## SOP-7. Reply to the resident
Reply to the resident with what will happen next, the rule that applies by its name and number, and any facts still needed. The reply does not promise a fine, a payment or an outcome.

## SOP-8. Hand over to the building manager
Hand the complaint over to the building manager when one of these applies, with a handover note:

- penalty_not_authorised: a fine, penalty or charge is asked for that no rule in the documents supports
- money_or_compensation: a resident's claim for compensation or a refund, or a payment the documents do not already settle. A question of who bears a cost that the documents assign, such as damage from renovation works, is answered from those documents
- legal_dispute: a threat of the Strata Management Tribunal, a lawyer or a court, or a disputed reading of a rule
- emergency: danger now, such as water gushing, fire, a lift entrapment or a collapse
- complaint_about_management: a complaint about how the management office, its staff, the guards or the committee behaved. A question whether a rule allows what staff did, such as asking a visitor for identification, is answered from the rule

Missing facts and a difference between documents are not reasons to hand over: ask for the facts, and state which document governs.

The handover note gives the reason, a summary of the complaint, the facts collected, the facts still missing, the rules cited, the steps already suggested and the urgency.

## Facts to collect by complaint type

| Complaint type | Facts to collect |
|---|---|
| noise | date and time, duration, unit believed to be the source, whether it has happened before |
| parking | bay number, vehicle registration number, date and time seen |
| pets | unit of the pet owner, what happened (noise, fouling, unleashed), date and time |
| renovation | unit where works are done, date and time, type of works (drilling, hacking) |
| leakage | location of the leak in the unit, whether water is still coming in, when it started, unit above, photographs |
| refuse | what was left, where, when first seen, owner if known |
| obstruction | items, corridor and floor, how long they have been there |
| facilities | facility, date and time, number of guests, parcel concerned |
| visitors_access | date and time, visitor's name, what the guard asked for |
| moving | requested date and time, date of notice given, deposit receipt |
| smoke_odour | type of smoke or smell, times, unit believed to be the source |
| damage_common_property | what was damaged, when, contractor and unit concerned, photographs |
| maintenance_repair | fault, location, date first reported, work order number if any |
| charges | parcel, month, amount billed, the item queried |
| penalty_query | the fine or charge, the date notified, the rule named in the notice |
| compensation | what was damaged, when, amount claimed |
| dispute | the decision disputed, the action threatened |
| management_conduct | staff member or post, date and time, what happened |
