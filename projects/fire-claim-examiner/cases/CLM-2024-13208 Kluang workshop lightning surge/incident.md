I am the managing director of Presisi Mesra Engineering Sdn Bhd at No. 7, Jalan Perindustrian Kluang 4. This afternoon there was a heavy thunderstorm. When we checked, the two CNC machines cannot start and show drive alarms, the air compressor motor smells burnt, the overhead crane control does not respond, and the main incoming breaker and the surge arrester in the switchboard are burnt. There was no fire and we did not call Bomba. Nobody was hurt.

Our electrical engineer is coming tomorrow morning. Please inform the insurance company.
