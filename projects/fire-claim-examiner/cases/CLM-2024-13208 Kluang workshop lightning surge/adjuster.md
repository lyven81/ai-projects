# Adjuster's report

**Seri Adjusters Sdn Bhd** · Our ref SAS/JH/24/1046 · Report dated 14 November 2024 · Adjuster: Elaine Ng Pei Shan

## Investigation

| Area | Finding |
|---|---|
| Site inspection | 22 October 2024. Workshop floor, main switchboard, CNC turning centre, CNC vertical machining centre, air compressor room and overhead crane inspected with the Insured's managing director and its electrical engineer; the roof of the adjoining unit, No. 9, viewed from the access ladder with the neighbour's consent. |
| Cause and origin | Lightning strike during a thunderstorm at about 3:41 pm on 17 October 2024. The strike point is the steel ridge finial on the roof of the adjoining unit, No. 9, about 15 metres from the Insured's switchboard: the finial tip is melted and blackened and the test joint of its down conductor is scorched. The strike induced a surge on the incoming low voltage supply and the shared earthing system, which passed through the main switchboard into the machines on the workshop floor. No fire ensued: there is no flame, charring or smoke damage outside the burnt components, and no fire was reported or attended. |
| Reports relied on | Malaysian Meteorological Department record for Kluang, 17 October 2024: thunderstorm from 2:50 pm to 4:30 pm. Malaysian Meteorological Department lightning detection record, 17 October 2024: cloud-to-ground strike at 3:41 pm located within 100 metres of the risk. Electrical engineer's report by Ir. Mohamad Firdaus bin Rahim, 21 October 2024: surge arrester module failed, incoming MCCB and power meter burnt; spindle drive, servo amplifiers, compressor motor windings and crane contactor panel damaged by overvoltage from a lightning-induced surge; no fire, no flame and no charring outside the components; the workshop wiring otherwise sound. CNC supplier's service reports, 22 October 2024: drive and control board failures consistent with a supply surge. Tenaga Nasional Berhad: a momentary disturbance recorded on the feeder at 3:41 pm, consistent with lightning; no network fault. |
| People interviewed | The managing director. The machinist and the store assistant who were on the workshop floor at the time. The electrical engineer who prepared the report. |
