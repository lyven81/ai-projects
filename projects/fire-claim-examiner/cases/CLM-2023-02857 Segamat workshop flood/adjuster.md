# Adjuster's report

**Seri Adjusters Sdn Bhd** · Our ref SAS/JH/23/0341 · Report dated 24 March 2023 · Adjuster: Low Wei Jie

## Investigation

| Area | Finding |
|---|---|
| Site inspection | 4 March 2023, after the water receded. Single-storey workshop, yard and rear store inspected with the Insured. Flood line on internal walls at 0.95 m. |
| Cause and origin | Overflow of Sungai Segamat after prolonged heavy rain from 28 February 2023. Water entered from the road and the rear drain and stood inside the workshop overnight on 1 to 2 March 2023. Roof sheets, ridge cap and walls intact; no opening made by wind. |
| Reports relied on | Department of Irrigation and Drainage river level records for Segamat, 28 February to 4 March 2023, river above danger level from the evening of 1 March. Segamat district disaster management notice of flood evacuation for Jalan Genuang, 1 March 2023. Electrical contractor's test report, 6 March 2023. |
| People interviewed | The Insured. His senior mechanic, who closed the workshop at 6:00 pm on 1 March. The owner of the adjoining tyre shop, flooded to the same depth. |

## Findings

| Area | Finding |
|---|---|
| Occupancy at the date of loss | Motor repair workshop trading six days a week. |
| Maintenance and prior defects | None relevant to the loss. |
| Risk as found against the schedule | Single-storey detached workshop used for motor vehicle servicing and mechanical repair, as scheduled. No spray painting booth, spray guns or paint mixing found on the premises. |
| Damage found per item | Wall plaster, roller shutter motor and power points below the flood line; compressor, hoist motor and tools water damaged; spare parts on the lower racks rusted and contaminated. A customer's vehicle left for repair was flooded inside the workshop. |
| Discrepancies with the incident report | The Insured describes strong wind on the night. The roof and walls show no wind damage, and no water entered through the roof. The damage was caused by water entering from outside at ground level. |
| Irregularities | None found. |
