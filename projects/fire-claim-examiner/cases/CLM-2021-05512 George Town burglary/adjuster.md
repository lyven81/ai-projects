# Adjuster's report

**Mutiara Loss Adjusters Sdn Bhd** · Our ref MLA/PG/21/0418 · Report dated 20 August 2021 · Adjuster: Tan Boon Keat

## Investigation

| Area | Finding |
|---|---|
| Site inspection | 9 August 2021. Ground floor shop, rear store, front counter and rear service door inspected with the Insured. |
| Cause and origin | Forced entry at the rear service door overnight between about 10:00 pm on 14 July and 6:30 am on 15 July 2021. The folding steel grille was levered up at the lower track and the door opened. Goods taken from the shelves and the rear store; the cash drawer under the front counter prised open. No sign of fire, smoke, heat or explosion anywhere on the premises. |
| Reports relied on | Police report GTN/007744/21, Balai Polis Patani Road, lodged 15 July 2021 at 9:12 am: forced entry and theft of goods and cash, under investigation, no fire recorded. Supplier invoices for cigarettes and infant formula, June 2021. The Insured's stock listing, undated. The Insured's handwritten daily takings record, 12 to 14 July 2021. Quotation for door and grille repair, 3 August 2021. |
| People interviewed | The Insured. Her son, who suggested the claim to her. |

## Findings

| Area | Finding |
|---|---|
| Occupancy at the date of loss | Provision shop trading daily; first floor residence occupied by the Insured and her family throughout. |
| Maintenance and prior defects | None relevant to the loss. |
| Risk as found against the schedule | Two-storey intermediate shophouse, no mezzanine; provision shop on the ground floor, residence above. Rear service door fitted with a folding steel grille. No change of trade or occupation and no transfer of interest. |
| Damage found per item | Rear service door, frame and folding grille forced and bent; counter drawer and lock broken. Shelves and rear store partly emptied. No other damage to the building. |
| Discrepancies with the incident report | The Insured states the shop has been insured with the Company for several years under annual policies. She produced only the schedule for 30 July 2021 to 29 July 2022. No schedule for any earlier period was produced by her or located on the Company's file. |
| Irregularities | None found. |
