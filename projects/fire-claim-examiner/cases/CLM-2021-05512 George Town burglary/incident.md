**Subject:** Shop at 88 Lebuh Chulia broken into

Dear Ms Cheah,

I am Ooi Bee Choo, I run the provision shop at 88, Lebuh Chulia, George Town. My son is helping me write this email.

My shop was broken into on the night of 14 July. I closed the shop at about 10 pm on 14 July and went upstairs, we stay on the first floor. When I came down at about 6.30 am on 15 July, the back service door was open, the folding steel grille was forced up at the bottom track and the shop was in a mess.

They took cigarettes, canned food, cooking oil and infant formula from the shelves and from the back store. The cash drawer under the front counter was prised open and emptied. Inside was about RM 9,500 in notes and coins, my takings from 12 to 14 July that I had not banked in yet.

I made a police report at Balai Polis Patani Road the same morning at 9.12 am, report number GTN/007744/21. There was no fire, no smoke, nothing like that at any time. Only the back door, its frame and the grille were damaged.

I did not inform you earlier because I thought this is only a police matter. Last week my son said maybe the shop policy can claim, so I am writing to you now. The shop has been insured with the same company for many years, every year renew. I do not keep the old schedules, only the current one.

Please let me know what to do.

Thank you,
Ooi Bee Choo
