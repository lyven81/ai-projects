# Adjuster's report

**Bayu Loss Adjusters Sdn Bhd** · Our ref BLA/SL/24/0094 · Report dated 23 February 2024 · Adjuster: Chan Kok Leong

## Investigation

| Area | Finding |
|---|---|
| Site inspection | 30 January 2024, after Bomba released the site. Whole warehouse inspected with the Insured; the rear bay inspected with the sub-tenant's supervisor. |
| Cause and origin | Fire began at an electrically driven tyre shredding machine in the rear bay, which was in use until about 9:00 pm. Smouldering rubber crumb at the discharge chute ignited the stockpile of used tyres around it. The fire spread through the rear bay and roof into the front bay where the Insured's garment stock was held. |
| Reports relied on | Bomba fire investigation report, 28 January 2024: fire originated at the tyre shredder in the rear bay; heavy fire load from about 3,000 used tyres and rubber crumb. Police report at Klang district police headquarters, 28 January 2024. Sublease agreement between the Insured and Rubber Kitar Enterprise dated 1 September 2023, produced by the Insured. Klang municipal council business licence record for Rubber Kitar Enterprise at the risk address, issued October 2023, activity: recycling of used tyres. |
| People interviewed | The Insured. The supervisor of Rubber Kitar Enterprise, who confirmed shredding operations in the rear bay from October 2023, five days a week. Two neighbouring occupiers. |

## Findings

| Area | Finding |
|---|---|
| Occupancy at the date of loss | Front bay used by the Insured for storage of finished garments. Rear bay, about half the floor area, occupied since September 2023 by the sub-tenant for storing used tyres and shredding them into rubber crumb. The two bays are under one roof with no fire wall between them. |
| Maintenance and prior defects | No fire detection installed in the rear bay. Rubber crumb accumulated around the shredder. |
| Risk as found against the schedule | Scheduled as a warehouse for the storage of finished garments. Found partly used for tyre storage and mechanical shredding of tyres from September and October 2023, about four months before the loss. No endorsement on the policy records this use. |
| Damage found per item | Roof sheets and trusses collapsed over the rear bay and two-thirds of the front bay; walls spalled; wiring destroyed. Garment cartons in the front bay burnt or soaked by fire-fighting water; cartons nearest the front door salvageable. |
| Discrepancies with the incident report | The Insured describes the rear bay as rented to 'the other company' to 'keep their things'. The sub-tenant operated a tyre shredding process there, not storage only. |
| Irregularities | None found as to the amounts claimed. The Insured signed the sublease and received rent from September 2023. |
