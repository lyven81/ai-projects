# Adjuster's report

**Kenari Loss Adjusters Sdn Bhd** · Our ref KLA/TR/24/0288 · Report dated 12 June 2024 · Adjuster: Kavitha a/p Ramasamy

## Investigation

| Area | Finding |
|---|---|
| Site inspection | 24 May 2024. Roof inspected from a ladder and from inside the roof space with the Insured's contractor; upstairs rooms inspected. |
| Cause and origin | Rainwater overflowed the rear box gutter, which was blocked with leaves and silt, and ran back under the bottom row of tiles into the roof space above the rear bedroom and corridor. Further water was driven in through gaps in the deteriorated sealant around the rear bedroom window frame. All roof tiles were in place; no tile, ridge or wall was displaced or broken by the wind. |
| Reports relied on | Malaysian Meteorological Department record for Kuala Terengganu, 21 May 2024: thunderstorm with heavy rain from 6:45 pm, maximum gust 48 km/h. Roofing contractor's inspection note, 24 May 2024: gutter blocked, tiles intact, two tiles with old hairline cracks. |
| People interviewed | The Insured. Her roofing contractor. The neighbour at No. 11, whose house had no damage. |

## Findings

| Area | Finding |
|---|---|
| Occupancy at the date of loss | Occupied by the Insured and her family as their home. |
| Maintenance and prior defects | Rear box gutter not cleared for some time: compacted leaves and silt found along its length. Window frame sealant cracked and partly missing. The two cracked tiles at the rear show lichen growth in the cracks, consistent with cracking well before the storm; the roof space below them was dry. |
| Risk as found against the schedule | Two-storey intermediate terrace house used as a private dwelling, as scheduled. |
| Damage found per item | Water-stained and collapsed ceiling boards in the rear bedroom and corridor; stained paintwork; wet mattress, swollen wardrobe panels, stained carpet and curtains. |
| Discrepancies with the incident report | The Insured believes the wind blew off roof tiles. No tile was missing or displaced; the water entered through the overflowing gutter and the window frame gaps, not through any opening made by the wind. |
| Irregularities | None found. |
