# Adjuster's report

**Mutiara Loss Adjusters Sdn Bhd** · Our ref MLA/PH/25/0733 · Report dated 8 September 2025 · Adjuster: Rajesh Kumar a/l Subramaniam

## Investigation

| Area | Finding |
|---|---|
| Site inspection | 15 August 2025. Ground floor pharmacy and first floor office, store and washroom inspected with the managing pharmacist; the opened ceiling void below the washroom inspected. |
| Cause and origin | A brass compression fitting on the 15 mm cold water supply to the first floor washroom basin, in the ceiling void above the ground floor, split along its body. Water discharged into the void through the night and brought down the ceiling boards over the front half of the pharmacy. Discharge until the main valve was closed at about 7:35 am. |
| Reports relied on | Plumber's report by Paip Pantai Timur Enterprise, 14 August 2025: fitting split, no corrosion on the adjoining pipework, fitting replaced. Plumbing service record by the same contractor, 10 June 2025: washroom supply checked, no defects or leaks noted. The building has no automatic sprinkler installation. |
| People interviewed | The managing pharmacist. The pharmacy assistant who closed the shop at 10:00 pm on 13 August 2025. The plumbing contractor. |

## Findings

| Area | Finding |
|---|---|
| Occupancy at the date of loss | Pharmacy trading daily, 9:00 am to 10:00 pm, occupied by the Insured as its own premises. Closed overnight at the time of the discharge. |
| Maintenance and prior defects | Plumbing serviced by contract. No defect in the washroom supply was reported before the loss, and none was found on the pipework next to the failed fitting. |
| Risk as found against the schedule | Two-storey intermediate shophouse: ground floor pharmacy, first floor office, store and washroom. Two storeys in total; no mezzanine floor; no further floors. Construction as found: brick walls, reinforced concrete first floor slab, concrete tile roof on timber trusses. |
| Damage found per item | Ceiling boards collapsed over the front half of the ground floor; wall plaster wet to about 400 mm; two display shelves and the front counter with swollen particleboard; stock on the lower shelves wetted. |
| Discrepancies with the incident report | None material. Item 1 reduced on measurement: the rear store ceiling was not affected. |
| Irregularities | None found. |
