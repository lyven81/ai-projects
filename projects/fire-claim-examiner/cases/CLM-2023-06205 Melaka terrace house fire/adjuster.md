# Adjuster's report

**Cahaya Adjusters Sdn Bhd** · Our ref CAS/ML/23/0655 · Report dated 5 July 2023 · Adjuster: Deepa a/p Murugan

## Investigation

| Area | Finding |
|---|---|
| Site inspection | 13 June 2023. House inspected with the Insured; tenant's remaining belongings and the mail box inspected. |
| Cause and origin | Fire began at the double power socket behind the refrigerator in the kitchen; the socket and plug show arcing damage. Fire spread to the kitchen cabinets and up into the rear roof. |
| Reports relied on | Bomba report, 10 June 2023: fire of electrical origin at the kitchen socket, house found locked and empty. Police report at Melaka Tengah district police headquarters, 10 June 2023. Tenaga Nasional Berhad billing for the address: 312 kWh in March 2023, 61 kWh in April 2023, 9 kWh in May 2023. Water bills for the same months: 18, 6 and 0 cubic metres. |
| People interviewed | The Insured. The tenant, Encik Kamal Arif bin Zainal, by telephone on 14 June 2023 and in a signed statement on 20 June 2023: he moved to Johor Bahru for a new job on 15 April 2023, took most of his belongings, left the refrigerator running, and had not returned to the house since; he kept paying the rent and intended to end the tenancy at the end of June. Neighbours at Nos. 35 and 39: no one seen at the house since mid April; mail and flyers piling at the gate. |

## Findings

| Area | Finding |
|---|---|
| Occupancy at the date of loss | Let under a tenancy from 1 January 2023, but no one living in or attending the house from 15 April 2023 to the date of loss, 56 days. Verified against the tenant's signed statement, the electricity and water consumption, the uncollected mail and the neighbours' accounts. |
| Maintenance and prior defects | None reported to the Insured. |
| Risk as found against the schedule | Single-storey intermediate terrace house let as a private dwelling, as scheduled, but unoccupied at the date of loss. |
| Damage found per item | Kitchen, rear roof and rear ceiling destroyed; wiring in the rear half burnt; smoke damage throughout. Landlord's kitchen cabinets and dining set destroyed; beds and wardrobes smoke damaged. |
| Discrepancies with the incident report | The Insured states the tenant was staying in the house at the time. The tenant confirms he had not lived there since 15 April 2023. The Insured was not aware of this: the rent continued to be paid and the tenant had not told her. |
| Irregularities | None found as to the amounts claimed. |
