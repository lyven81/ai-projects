# Adjuster's report

**Mutiara Loss Adjusters Sdn Bhd** · Our ref MLA/PH/25/0391 · Report dated 21 May 2025 · Adjuster: Amirul Hakim bin Zainal

## Investigation

| Area | Finding |
|---|---|
| Site inspection | 25 April 2025. House, distribution board, service cable entry, appliances and the rear compound inspected with the Insured; the coconut palm at the rear of the neighbouring lot, No. 14, inspected the same day with the neighbour's consent. |
| Cause and origin | Lightning strike during a thunderstorm at about 5:08 pm on 22 April 2025. The strike point is a coconut palm at the rear of No. 14, about 20 metres from the Insured's house: its crown is scorched and the bark is split in a continuous burnt strip down the trunk to the ground. The strike induced a surge on the overhead service cable to the house, which entered through the distribution board and the circuits beyond it and damaged the appliances and the electrical installation. No fire ensued: there is no flame, charring or smoke damage outside the burnt components, and no fire was reported or attended. |
| Reports relied on | Malaysian Meteorological Department record for Temerloh, 22 April 2025: thunderstorm from 4:35 pm to 6:20 pm. Malaysian Meteorological Department lightning detection record, 22 April 2025: cloud-to-ground strike at 5:08 pm located within 150 metres of the risk. Electrician's report by Temerloh Elektrik Jaya, registered wireman, 24 April 2025: RCCB and two MCBs burnt, porch lighting circuit cable insulation pitted at the external junction box; damage pattern consistent with a lightning-induced surge entering through the service cable; no fire. Appliance technician's reports, 25 April 2025, and laptop service centre report, 26 April 2025: main boards, control boards and power circuits burnt by overvoltage. Tenaga Nasional Berhad: no network fault recorded on the feeder that afternoon and supply not interrupted. |
| People interviewed | The Insured. Her neighbour at No. 14, whose television was also damaged. The registered wireman who prepared the electrician's report. |

Laptop power circuit and motherboard burnt while on charge. Wifi router and pay-TV decoder burnt. No damage to the building fabric and no fire. |
