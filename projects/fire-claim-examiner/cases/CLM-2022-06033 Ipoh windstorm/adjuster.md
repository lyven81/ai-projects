# Adjuster's report

**Kenari Loss Adjusters Sdn Bhd** · Our ref KLA/PK/22/0367 · Report dated 22 July 2022 · Adjuster: Siti Hajar binti Rosli

## Investigation

| Area | Finding |
|---|---|
| Site inspection | 5 July 2022. Main roof from a ladder and from the roof space, rear bedroom, passage, car porch and front boundary wall inspected with the claimant. Tarpaulin over the rear roof slope in place since 1 July 2022. |
| Cause and origin | Thunderstorm with strong wind on the late afternoon of 30 June 2022. The wind lifted and displaced a section of clay tiles on the rear slope of the main roof, broke two timber battens and dislodged part of the rear gable coping. Rain then entered the roof space through that opening and came through the ceilings of the rear bedroom and the passage. Separately, in the same storm, a branch broke off a mature rain tree standing on the neighbouring vacant lot and fell across the car porch. |
| Reports relied on | Malaysian Meteorological Department record, Ipoh station, 30 June 2022: thunderstorm, maximum gusts 68 km/h between 5:20 pm and 6:15 pm. Local council removal notice for the fallen branch, 2 July 2022. Contractor quotations for the roof and ceiling and for the car porch, 6 July 2022. The claimant's photographs, timestamped 30 June and 1 July 2022. Title search: the Insured is the registered owner. |
| People interviewed | The claimant. The Insured's spouse, at home during the storm. The roofing contractor who laid the tarpaulin on 1 July 2022. |

## Findings

| Area | Finding |
|---|---|
| Occupancy at the date of loss | Dwelling occupied throughout; the Insured's spouse was at home at the time of the storm. |
| Maintenance and prior defects | Remaining tiles and battens on the rear slope sound. No old water staining in the roof space outside the area below the opening. |
| Risk as found against the schedule | Single-storey detached dwelling with an attached car porch roofed in translucent sheeting, as scheduled. No change of occupation or use and no transfer of interest. |
| Damage found per item | Sequence confirmed on site: tiles missing and two battens broken on the rear slope directly above the wetted ceilings, with water staining running from the opening to the collapsed boards. Ceiling boards down in the rear bedroom and passage; bedroom floor finishes and skirting, wardrobe, mattress and bedding wetted. Car porch: three translucent sheets cracked and the gutter dented beneath where the branch fell. The metal signboard on the front boundary wall and the retractable fabric awning over the front windows torn from their fixings by the wind. |
| Discrepancies with the incident report | None material. |
| Irregularities | None found. |
