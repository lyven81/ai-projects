# Adjuster's report

**Teratai Adjusters Sdn Bhd** · Our ref TAS/PK/23/1102 · Report dated 15 December 2023 · Adjuster: Arumugam a/l Velu

## Investigation

| Area | Finding |
|---|---|
| Site inspection | 5 December 2023. Both floors inspected. Ground floor retail area: ceiling boards collapsed at the rear, plaster and skirting saturated to about 600 mm. First floor washroom: the replaced section of supply pipe and the opened wall cavity. |
| Cause and origin | Failure of a soldered joint on the concealed 15 mm copper supply pipe to the first floor washroom. The removed section, retained by the plumber, shows heavy corrosion at and around the joint. Water ran down the cavity into the ground floor ceiling void. |
| Reports relied on | Plumber's job sheet, 2 December 2023: joint failed, adjacent pipework heavily corroded, section replaced. Inspection report by Syarikat Paip Aman, 19 September 2023: corrosion on exposed washroom pipework, concealed sections likely similar, replacement recommended as soon as practicable. |
| People interviewed | The Insured: confirmed the lot has been vacant since 15 October, weekly visits, repair deferred until a new tenant was found. The plumber: the failed joint lies on the same washroom pipework the September report described. |

## Findings

| Area | Finding |
|---|---|
| Occupancy at the date of loss | Ground floor untenanted from 15 October 2023 to 2 December 2023, 48 days, verified against the tenancy handover acknowledgement and the letting advertisement. No person in daily occupation of either floor; the Insured attended about weekly. |
| Maintenance and prior defects | Corrosion on the washroom supply pipework was identified on 19 September 2023 by a contractor the Insured engaged. Quotation obtained 6 October 2023. No repair carried out before the loss. The failure occurred on that pipework. |
| Risk as found against the schedule | Two-storey intermediate shophouse, no mezzanine, as scheduled. No sprinkler installation. No change of trade or construction; the only change found is the vacancy of the ground floor. |
| Damage found per item | Ceiling, plaster and skirting on the ground floor; stationery cartons and office files stored at floor level; two desks and one filing cabinet with swollen particleboard. No damage to the first floor beyond the replaced pipe. |
| Discrepancies with the incident report | None material. The Insured's account of the vacancy, the September inspection and the deferred repair is borne out by the documents seen. |
| Irregularities | None found. Stationery invoices reconcile with the stock seen on site; the contractor quotation is consistent with the damage. |
