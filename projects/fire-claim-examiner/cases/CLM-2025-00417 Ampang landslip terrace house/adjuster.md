# Adjuster's report

**Teratai Adjusters Sdn Bhd** · Our ref TAS/SL/25/0021 · Report dated 31 January 2025 · Adjuster: Khairul Anuar bin Mustafa

## Investigation

| Area | Finding |
|---|---|
| Site inspection | 7 January 2025, with the Insured and his geotechnical engineer; the slope behind the row inspected from the rear lane. |
| Cause and origin | Shallow landslip of the cut slope behind Nos. 21 to 27 after prolonged rainfall from 29 December 2024. The slipped soil mass pushed the rear retaining wall of the lot over and moved the ground beneath the rear external wall of the house: the strip footing under that wall settled about 40 mm and rotated, cracking the wall and the kitchen floor slab which rests on it. |
| Reports relied on | Ampang Jaya Municipal Council inspection letter, 6 January 2025: slope failure behind the row, rear areas of four houses cordoned. Geotechnical engineer's report by Ir. Ng Kian Seng, 11 January 2025: rain-induced slope failure; the house foundations were built to the approved drawings and are not defective; the slope is not near any river or the coast; no demolition, alteration or repair works were under way. Department of Irrigation and Drainage rainfall records for Ampang, 29 December 2024 to 4 January 2025. |
| People interviewed | The Insured. The engineer. The owners of Nos. 23 and 25, whose rear walls are also cracked. |

## Findings

| Area | Finding |
|---|---|
| Occupancy at the date of loss | Occupied by the Insured and his family as their home; the rear kitchen cordoned off since the loss, the family living in the front of the house. |
| Maintenance and prior defects | None relevant to the rear of the house. The front driveway shows hairline and 3 mm cracks with dirt and moss in them, consistent with settlement of the driveway fill over several years; the engineer finds no ground movement at the front of the lot on 4 January 2025. |
| Risk as found against the schedule | Two-storey end terrace house used as a private dwelling, as scheduled. |
| Damage found per item | Rear external wall cracked through with the footing beneath it settled; kitchen floor slab cracked and dropped at the rear edge; kitchen cabinets and tiles cracked by the movement; rear retaining wall overturned. Front driveway cracks not caused by this event. |
| Discrepancies with the incident report | The claim includes the front driveway; its cracking predates the loss and is unrelated to the slip at the rear. |
| Irregularities | None found. |
