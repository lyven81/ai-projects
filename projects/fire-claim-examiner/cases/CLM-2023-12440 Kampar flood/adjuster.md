# Adjuster's report

**Bayu Loss Adjusters Sdn Bhd** · Our ref BLA/PK/24/0058 · Report dated 9 February 2024 · Adjuster: Wong Kar Mun

## Investigation

| Area | Finding |
|---|---|
| Site inspection | 23 January 2024, about one month after the water receded. Ground floor shop, rear room, rear boundary wall and paved rear driveway inspected with the claimant. Flood line on internal walls at about 1.3 m. Most damaged stock already discarded; seen in the claimant's photographs. |
| Cause and origin | Overflow of Sungai Kampar after continuous heavy rain from the afternoon of 19 December 2023. Water entered the ground floor from the rear lane at about 3:00 am on 20 December 2023, rose to about 1.2 m and stood until the afternoon of 21 December. As the water receded, a section of the rear boundary wall about four metres long leaned and partly collapsed and the paved rear driveway settled unevenly, the ground beneath having been saturated and washed out. The main building shows no cracking or settlement. |
| Reports relied on | Department of Irrigation and Drainage flood bulletin, Kampar district, 20 December 2023: Sungai Kampar above the danger threshold from 1:40 am to 4:00 pm. Contractor quotations for building reinstatement and for the boundary wall and driveway, 15 January 2024. Supplier invoices for stock, September to December 2023. The claimant's handwritten daily takings record, 16 to 19 December 2023. Purchase receipts for the chest freezer (2021) and display chiller (2022). The claimant's photographs, timestamped 20 to 23 December 2023. Title search: the Insured is the registered proprietor. |
| People interviewed | The claimant. The contractor who quoted for the boundary wall and driveway. |

## Findings

| Area | Finding |
|---|---|
| Occupancy at the date of loss | Shop trading; first floor residence occupied throughout by the claimant and her two children. The Insured works in Singapore and returns monthly. |
| Maintenance and prior defects | None relevant to the loss. |
| Risk as found against the schedule | Two-storey intermediate shophouse, no mezzanine, as scheduled; rear boundary wall and paved rear driveway within the lot, not separately itemised. No change of trade or occupation. |
| Damage found per item | Wall plaster, skirting, doors and electrical points below about 1.5 m; shop cabinetry and shelving soaked; chest freezer, display chiller and point of sale terminal water damaged; stock on lower shelving and at floor level destroyed; clothing and household linen in the ground floor rear room destroyed; rear boundary wall leaning and partly collapsed over about four metres; rear driveway settled. Silt throughout the ground floor. |
| Discrepancies with the incident report | None material. |
| Irregularities | First notice of the loss and the written claim both reached the Company on 20 January 2024, 31 days after the loss. No request for more time was made to the Company before that date and no written extension of time appears on the file. Reasons given by the claimant: no electricity or mobile coverage for several days, cleaning, the children's school arrangements, and the Insured in Singapore until the new year. |
