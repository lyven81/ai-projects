**Subject:** Fire at the bakery, 12 Jalan Badlishah

Dear Puan Rosnah,

I am Nurul Ain, daughter of Encik Mohd Faizal bin Abdul Rahman. I manage his bakery at 12, Jalan Badlishah, Sungai Petani. Early yesterday morning, 14 September, there was a fire in the bakery. Sorry I am only writing now, yesterday the whole day was with Bomba and the clean-up.

We closed at about 10 pm on Friday after the evening production run and all the staff left by 10.30 pm. Nobody stays upstairs; it is only our store and office.

At about 1.40 am a passer-by saw smoke coming from the shopfront and called Bomba. They came at 1.58 am and the fire was out by 3.12 am. Bomba said it started at the spiral dough mixer against the back wall of the production area, maybe electrical. From there it spread to the timber shelves and the packaging, then across the production area. The deck oven, the proofer and the two stainless steel worktables are damaged, and part of the ceiling downstairs is burnt through. Soot everywhere downstairs and on the lower staircase; upstairs only smoke stains. All the flour, sugar, butter, fillings and packaging are spoilt.

Nobody was hurt. My father asked me to handle this for him. Please inform the insurance company.

Thank you,
Nurul Ain binti Mohd Faizal
