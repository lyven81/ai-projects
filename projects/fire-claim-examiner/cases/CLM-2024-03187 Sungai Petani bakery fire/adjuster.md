# Adjuster's report

**Cahaya Adjusters Sdn Bhd** · Our ref CAS/KD/24/0871 · Report dated 4 October 2024 · Adjuster: Zulkifli bin Hashim

## Investigation

| Area | Finding |
|---|---|
| Site inspection | 17 September 2024. Production area, ground floor store, shopfront, internal staircase and the first floor store and office inspected with the claimant. The mixer, deck oven, proofer and worktables retained on site. |
| Cause and origin | Electrical fault within the motor of the spiral dough mixer standing against the rear wall of the production area. Fire spread from the mixer to the adjacent timber shelving and packaging materials, then across the production area, and burnt through part of the ground floor ceiling. Smoke and soot throughout the ground floor and the lower staircase; smoke staining only on the first floor. |
| Reports relied on | Fire and Rescue Department report, 19 September 2024: attended at 1:58 am, extinguished at 3:12 am; probable cause an electrical fault; severe localised burning at the mixer with fire spread outward from that position. Independent electrical report by a registered competent person, 23 September 2024: the mixer's motor windings show short circuiting and overheating consistent with an insulation failure within the motor, and the motor's own damage is attributable to that fault rather than to external burning; the premises wiring, distribution board and other machines showed no pre-existing defect, and their damage is consistent with exposure to the fire. Valuation report by a registered valuer, 24 September 2024. Local authority health officer's disposal record, 16 September 2024. |
| People interviewed | The claimant. The registered competent person who prepared the electrical report. |

## Findings

| Area | Finding |
|---|---|
| Occupancy at the date of loss | Bakery with on-site production, trading at this address since 2016. Closed after the evening production run on 13 September 2024; all staff left by 10:30 pm. Nobody resides on the first floor, which is storage and office. |
| Maintenance and prior defects | No pre-existing defect in the premises wiring, distribution board or the other machines (electrical report of 23 September 2024). |
| Risk as found against the schedule | Two-storey intermediate shophouse, no mezzanine; bakery and confectionery production on the ground floor, storage and office on the first floor, as scheduled. No change of trade or occupation and no transfer of interest: the Insured remains the registered proprietor of the premises and sole proprietor of the business. |
| Damage found per item | Spiral dough mixer destroyed, motor burnt out from within. Deck oven, dough proofer and two stainless steel worktables destroyed or heat damaged by the spreading fire. Timber shelving and packaging store fittings burnt. Ground floor ceiling burnt through in part; wall finishes, affected circuits and the rear roof section damaged; soot throughout the ground floor. Stock of flour, sugar, butter, fillings and packaging destroyed by fire, heat or contamination. |
| Discrepancies with the incident report | None material. |
| Irregularities | None found. |
