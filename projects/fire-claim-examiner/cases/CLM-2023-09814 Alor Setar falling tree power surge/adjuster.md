# Adjuster's report

**Kenari Loss Adjusters Sdn Bhd** · Our ref KLA/KD/23/0461 · Report dated 13 October 2023 · Adjuster: Faridah binti Osman

## Investigation

| Area | Finding |
|---|---|
| Site inspection | 22 September 2023. House, compound, roof, boundary wall and gate inspected with the Insured; the fallen tree site on Jalan Langgar inspected the same day. |
| Cause and origin | An angsana tree on the road reserve of Jalan Langgar, about 350 metres from the risk, fell across the overhead low voltage lines at about 3:30 pm. The fall caused a fault on the feeder serving Taman Uda; the resulting overvoltage damaged electrical equipment in the Insured's house and in other houses on the same feeder. The tree did not touch the Insured's house, compound, walls, gate or fence; no branch or other object from it reached the property. |
| Reports relied on | Tenaga Nasional Berhad outage record for the Taman Uda feeder, 19 September 2023: fault due to a fallen tree on the overhead line, supply restored at 6:55 pm. Alor Setar City Council record of the tree removal, 19 September 2023: roadside tree with root decay. Weather: Malaysian Meteorological Department record for Alor Setar shows no thunderstorm or strong wind that afternoon. Technician reports dated 25 September 2023 and 26 September 2023: damage consistent with an electrical surge. |
| People interviewed | The Insured. His neighbours at Nos. 19 and 23, whose appliances were also damaged. The city council officer in charge of the tree removal. |

## Findings

| Area | Finding |
