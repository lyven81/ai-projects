# Adjuster's report

**Seri Adjusters Sdn Bhd** · Our ref SAS/PK/25/0219 · Report dated 31 March 2025 · Adjuster: Thinesh a/l Rajan

## Investigation

| Area | Finding |
|---|---|
| Site inspection | 12 March 2025. House inspected with the Insured; front grille, door, living room, master bedroom inspected. |
| Cause and origin | Entry by night through the front: the grille padlock hasp was cut and the main door lock forced with a lever. Inside, the intruders searched the master bedroom, broke the wardrobe mirror doors, took jewellery from a drawer and cash from the dressing table, threw the television to the floor and sprayed paint on the living room wall before leaving. |
| Reports relied on | Police report lodged by the Insured at Sitiawan district police headquarters, 9 March 2025: house broken into by night and property stolen; investigation opened as housebreaking by night and theft. Police forensic attendance, 9 March 2025: tool marks on the grille and door lock. |
| People interviewed | The Insured and his wife. The neighbour at No. 16, who heard banging at about 2:00 am on 9 March 2025 but did not look out. |
