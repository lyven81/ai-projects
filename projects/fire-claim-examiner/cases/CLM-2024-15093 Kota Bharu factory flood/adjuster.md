# Adjuster's report

**Teratai Adjusters Sdn Bhd** · Our ref TAS/KB/24/2290 · Report dated 23 December 2024 · Adjuster: Mohd Hafiz bin Yusof

## Investigation

| Area | Finding |
|---|---|
| Site inspection | 4 December 2024, two days after the water receded. Single-storey factory and front yard inspected with the Insured. Flood line on internal walls at 0.85 m. |
| Cause and origin | River overflow. Water entered from the rear drainage reserve and the front yard and stood inside the factory from the afternoon of 29 November to 2 December 2024. No rainwater entry through the roof; roof and walls intact. |
| Reports relied on | Department of Irrigation and Drainage river level records for the Pengkalan Chepa station, 27 November to 3 December 2024, showing the river above danger level. Kota Bharu district flood relief notice closing the industrial area road on 29 November 2024. Machinery contractor's inspection report dated 7 December 2024. |
| People interviewed | The Insured. The factory supervisor, who was on site until 1:30 pm on 29 November. The occupier of the adjoining Lot 23, flooded to the same depth. |

## Findings

| Area | Finding |
|---|---|
| Occupancy at the date of loss | Factory in operation, five-day week, eleven workers. |
| Maintenance and prior defects | None relevant to the loss. |
| Risk as found against the schedule | Single-storey detached factory with a mezzanine office, used for producing and packing fish crackers, as scheduled. |
| Damage found per item | Wall plaster, timber doors and power points below the flood line; three machines with water-damaged motors and controls; floor-level stock and packaging soaked and contaminated. The free-standing signboard at the front gate was undermined by the water and toppled. |
| Discrepancies with the incident report | None material. The building claim included rewiring above the flood line, which was not affected. |
| Irregularities | None found. |
