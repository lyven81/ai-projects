# Adjuster's report

**Bayu Loss Adjusters Sdn Bhd** · Our ref BLA/SW/24/0412 · Report dated 30 August 2024 · Adjuster: Juliana anak Nyipa

## Investigation

| Area | Finding |
|---|---|
| Site inspection | 18 July 2024, with the Insured; second visit 12 August 2024 after the engineer's report. |
| Cause and origin | A 3-tonne lorry owned by Syarikat Angkut Pantas Sdn Bhd, reversing along the service lane to deliver to the adjoining lot, struck the shopfront at the corner pillar. The shutter frame and glass panels gave way; the front display shelves were knocked over. The falling shutter frame struck the rear door of the Insured's van parked in front. |
| Reports relied on | Police report at Miri district police headquarters, 16 July 2024, lodged jointly by the Insured and the lorry driver; the driver admits reversing into the shopfront. Structural engineer's report by Ir. Wong Teck Leong, 5 August 2024: cover concrete spalled and one link exposed at the corner pillar, structurally repairable. Insurer's email to the agent, 26 July 2024, allowing the Insured further time to deliver the written claim to 16 August 2024. |
| People interviewed | The Insured. The lorry driver, Encik Hamdan bin Sapuan, an employee of the lorry owner. The shop assistant present at the time. |
