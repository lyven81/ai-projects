# Adjuster's report

**Bayu Loss Adjusters Sdn Bhd** · Our ref BLA/NS/24/0917 · Report dated 30 September 2024 · Adjuster: Azlan Shah bin Kamarudin

## Investigation

| Area | Finding |
|---|---|
| Site inspection | 10 September 2024. Ground floor restaurant and five-foot way inspected with the Insured. Broken glass and damaged furniture retained in the rear store. |
| Cause and origin | A fight between two groups of football supporters, about 20 persons, spilled from the street onto the five-foot way at about 11:30 pm on 8 September 2024. Chairs and tables were thrown at the shopfront; the glass panels and door were broken; the counter equipment was struck. During the commotion one person reached over the counter and removed cash from the till. No fire occurred. |
| Reports relied on | Police report lodged by the Insured at Seremban district police headquarters, 9 September 2024. Police statement to the press, 9 September 2024: a public disturbance involving about 20 persons after a football match, nine arrested, the scene cleared by 12:15 am. CCTV footage from the restaurant camera, 11:28 pm to 11:44 pm. |
| People interviewed | The Insured. The cashier on duty. Two waiters. The operator of the adjoining shop, whose glass door was also broken. |

## Findings

| Area | Finding |
|---|---|
| Occupancy at the date of loss | Restaurant trading, staff and customers present at the time. |
| Maintenance and prior defects | None relevant. |
| Risk as found against the schedule | Ground floor of a three-storey shophouse used as a restaurant, as scheduled. Commercial premises; no residential use. |
| Damage found per item | Four glass shopfront panels and the glass door broken; five tables and 22 chairs broken; POS terminal and display chiller struck and inoperable; drinks stock in the chiller broken. Cash taken from the till during the disturbance, seen on CCTV. |
| Discrepancies with the incident report | None material. |
| Irregularities | None found. The disturbance is confined to one street for about 45 minutes; police describe it as a fight between supporters, with no political or other organised motive. |
