# Adjuster's report

**Cahaya Adjusters Sdn Bhd** · Our ref CAS/BP/23/1187 · Report dated 4 September 2023 · Adjuster: Nadia binti Azman

## Investigation

| Area | Finding |
|---|---|
| Site inspection | 15 August 2023. Ground floor restaurant kitchen, back counter and dining area nearest the kitchen inspected with the Insured. |
| Cause and origin | Fire started at the wok burner on the main gas stove: overheated cooking oil ignited while the burner was left unattended. Flames entered the exhaust hood and ducting and spread across the kitchen ceiling. |
| Reports relied on | Bomba report dated 12 August 2023: accidental kitchen fire from overheated cooking oil, extinguished by 3:30 pm. Police report at Batu Pahat district police headquarters, 12 August 2023. |
| People interviewed | The Insured. The cook, who left the burner on low heat to fetch supplies from the rear store for about ten minutes. The kitchen helper who called Bomba. |

## Findings

| Area | Finding |
|---|---|
| Occupancy at the date of loss | Restaurant trading daily, 7:00 am to 10:00 pm, with an afternoon break. Staff on the premises at the time of the fire. |
| Maintenance and prior defects | Exhaust hood cleaned in June 2023 per contractor receipt. Gas piping and regulator intact after the fire. No explosion occurred. |
| Risk as found against the schedule | Ground floor of a two-storey shophouse used as a restaurant, as scheduled. No change of trade or occupation. |
| Damage found per item | Kitchen ceiling, wall tiles, hood and ducting burnt; two stoves, fryer and chiller destroyed; six tables and 24 chairs near the kitchen door burnt or heat warped; kitchen food stock destroyed. Cash drawer charred with partly burnt notes inside. Motorcycle parked inside the rear kitchen door burnt. Sales and receipt books burnt. |
| Discrepancies with the incident report | Item 1 claimed at RM 41,500.00 included a hood upgrade from galvanised to stainless steel; the existing hood was stainless steel, so the difference is a pricing correction, not betterment. Assessed at RM 38,000.00. |
| Irregularities | None found. |
