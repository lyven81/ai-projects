# Adjuster's report

**Seri Adjusters Sdn Bhd** · Our ref SAS/JH/25/0802 · Report dated 28 July 2025 · Adjuster: Gopal a/l Krishnan

## Investigation

| Area | Finding |
|---|---|
| Site inspection | 25 June 2025. Ground floor shop lot inspected with the Insured; burnt handsets in the display cabinets and the store drawer counted and their IMEI numbers recorded where legible. |
| Cause and origin | Fire began at a multi-port charging station on the front display counter; a lithium battery pack on charge overnight went into thermal runaway. Fire spread to the counter, cabinets and ceiling. |
| Reports relied on | Bomba report, 23 June 2025: accidental fire, battery on charge. Police report at Muar district police headquarters, 23 June 2025. Letter from Teknik Gadget Trading, 21 July 2025, with its billing system log. Bank statements of the Insured's business account, January to June 2025. |
| People interviewed | The Insured. The bank security guard who raised the alarm. The sales manager of Teknik Gadget Trading. The Insured's shop assistant. |

## Findings

| Area | Finding |
|---|---|
| Occupancy at the date of loss | Mobile phone retail shop trading daily, closed overnight. |
| Maintenance and prior defects | None relevant. |
| Risk as found against the schedule | Ground floor shop lot used for retail of mobile phones and accessories, as scheduled. |
| Damage found per item | Ceiling, wiring and shutter at the front burnt; counters and cabinets destroyed; 61 handsets and tablets and a quantity of accessories burnt. |
| Discrepancies with the incident report | The claim states heavy restocking in May and June 2025. The point of sale system shows stock at cost of RM 41,000.00 on 22 June 2025, and the handsets recovered match that record. |
| Irregularities | Four invoices from Teknik Gadget Trading, dated between 12 May and 14 June 2025 and totalling RM 55,000.00, were submitted with the claim. The supplier's billing log shows all four were created on 30 June 2025, seven days after the fire. The supplier's sales manager states they were issued at the Insured's request and that no goods were delivered against them. No payment for them appears in the Insured's bank statements. The IMEI numbers listed on them match none of the handsets recovered. The Insured, when shown the log, did not dispute the creation date. |
