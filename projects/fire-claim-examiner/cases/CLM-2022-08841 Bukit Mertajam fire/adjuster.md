# Adjuster's report

**Seri Adjusters Sdn Bhd** · Our ref SAS/PG/22/0590 · Report dated 2 September 2022 · Adjuster: Yeoh Kian Huat

## Investigation

| Area | Finding |
|---|---|
| Site inspection | 15 August 2022. Ground floor retail area and rear storeroom, internal staircase and first floor store inspected with the claimant; the collapsed rear roof viewed from the first floor and the rear lane. |
| Cause and origin | Fire in the rear storeroom on the ground floor in the early hours of 28 July 2022, with the heaviest burning at and around the distribution board. Heat and smoke travelled up the internal staircase to the first floor store. The rear section of the roof collapsed during firefighting. Smoke staining throughout the front retail area. |
| Reports relied on | Fire and Rescue Department report, 3 August 2022: attended at about 2:40 am, fire extinguished by 4:05 am; cause undetermined, burn patterns consistent with an origin at or near the distribution board. Contractor quotation for building reinstatement, 8 August 2022. Business registration transfer record, effective 1 March 2022. Bank standing instruction statement, January 2021 to July 2022. The Company's file for policy FP/PG/2021/004512: a renewal notice for the period following 31 January 2021 issued to the Insured's registered address; no renewal schedule, renewal endorsement or later policy on the file. |
| People interviewed | The claimant. The occupier of the adjoining lot, who noticed the smoke and called the Fire and Rescue Department. |

## Findings

| Area | Finding |
|---|---|
| Occupancy at the date of loss | Hardware and household goods shop, closed for business at the time of the fire. First floor used for stock storage. |
| Maintenance and prior defects | None established; the Fire and Rescue Department records the cause as undetermined. |
| Risk as found against the schedule | Two-storey intermediate shophouse, no mezzanine; ground floor retail, first floor stock storage. The business is now carried on by the claimant: the sole proprietorship passed from Tan Cheng Hock to Tan Wei Ming with effect from 1 March 2022. No endorsement recording a change of insured interest seen. |
| Damage found per item | Rear roof structure, tiles and trusses collapsed; ceiling and rear wall of the storeroom burnt; distribution board and storeroom circuits destroyed; storeroom shelving and stock burnt; stock on both floors smoke damaged; counters and fixtures in the retail area smoke and heat damaged. |
| Discrepancies with the incident report | The claimant believes the policy is current because the premium continues to be paid by bank standing instruction. The standing instruction statement is a bank record. No premium receipt signed by an official or appointed agent of the Company was produced, and no renewal schedule was located. The only schedule seen runs from 1 January 2021 to 31 January 2021. |
| Irregularities | None found. |
