Dear Encik Fadzli,

I am Tan Wei Ming, son of Mr Tan Cheng Hock. I run the hardware and household goods shop at 42, Jalan Pasar, Bukit Mertajam. Sorry for informing you only now; this past week I was busy with Bomba, the clean-up and the suppliers.

Last Thursday, 28 July, at about 2.15 am, a fire started in the back storeroom on the ground floor. The shop was closed. Our neighbour next door saw the smoke and called Bomba. They came at about 2.40 am and put out the fire by about 4.05 am. Nobody was hurt.

The shelving and stock in the storeroom are all burnt. Heat and smoke went up the inside staircase to the upstairs store, and the back part of the roof collapsed while Bomba was fighting the fire. The front shop is black with smoke all over.

The Bomba report came out yesterday. It says the cause is undetermined, but the burning pattern is at the distribution board in the storeroom. I think it was an electrical fault.

My father retired from the business in March this year and I have been running the shop in my own name since then. The fire insurance premium is still paid every year by bank standing instruction, so the policy should still be active. Please inform the insurance company.

Regards,
Tan Wei Ming
