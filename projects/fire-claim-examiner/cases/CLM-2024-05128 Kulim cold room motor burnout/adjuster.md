# Adjuster's report

**Cahaya Adjusters Sdn Bhd** · Our ref CAS/KD/24/0533 · Report dated 20 May 2024 · Adjuster: Ho Mei Fong

## Investigation

| Area | Finding |
|---|---|
| Site inspection | 24 April 2024. Warehouse, cold room, condensing unit and electrical control panel inspected with the Insured and his refrigeration technician. |
| Cause and origin | Single phasing: one phase of the incoming supply to the condensing unit was lost at a loose, overheated terminal in the motor starter. The compressor motor ran on two phases, overran and its windings burnt out at about 2:00 am on 23 April 2024 (time from the cold room temperature logger). The overload relay contacts had welded and did not trip in time. No fire occurred: there is no flame, charring or smoke damage outside the motor casing and the starter, and no fire was reported or attended. The burning smell reported came from the motor windings. |
| Reports relied on | Refrigeration technician's report by Utara Sejuk Engineering, 23 April 2024: compressor motor burnt out, starter terminal L2 overheated and loose, no fire. Electrical engineer's report by Ir. Suresh a/l Pillai, 26 April 2024: failure caused by single phasing and overrunning; no fire; no lightning involvement, and the building's surge arrester shows no operation. Malaysian Meteorological Department record for Kulim, 22 to 23 April 2024: no thunderstorm or lightning activity. Kulim district health office letter, 25 April 2024, supervising disposal of thawed stock. |
| People interviewed | The Insured. The worker who opened the warehouse at 7:00 am. The refrigeration technician. |
