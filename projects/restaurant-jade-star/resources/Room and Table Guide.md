## G2 Which room fits which party
1 to 6 guests: Orchid Room or Rose Room. 7 to 8 guests: Tulip Room or Lotus Room. 9 to 10 guests: Sakura Room or Sunflower Room. When both rooms of that size are taken, a room of the next size up is offered; a party is never seated in a smaller room. More than 10 guests: the restaurant manager advises.
