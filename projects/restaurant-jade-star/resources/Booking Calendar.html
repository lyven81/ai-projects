K1 Booking season

1 to 31 December 2026, every day.

K2 Hours and slots

Open 10am to 10pm daily. Booking slots: 10am to 1pm, 1pm to 4pm, 4pm to 7pm, 7pm to 10pm. A time that is not one of the four slot starts cannot be booked.

K3 Cut-off

The last moment to book a slot is 2 hours before it starts: 8am for 10am, 11am for 1pm, 2pm for 4pm, 5pm for 7pm.

K4 Hold

A held room waits 15 minutes for the customer's confirmation, then is released.
