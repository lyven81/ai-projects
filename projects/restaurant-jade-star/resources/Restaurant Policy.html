Restaurant Policy (R1)

Jade Star Chinese Dining 吉星饭厅. Sample data. The rules every booking route follows: the website form, the chat with the restaurant assistant, and customer agents through A2A. This policy covers the booking only, from the request to the confirmed booking.

Resource: policy://restaurant, served by the MCP server to the restaurant assistant. Each clause has its own link, for example #P1.

1. Clauses

P1 Season

Bookings are for December 2026 only, 1 to 31 December. A request for any other date goes to the restaurant manager.

P2 Business hours

Open 10am to 10pm, 7 days a week. No booking outside business hours.

P3 Booking slots

Four booking slots a day: 10am, 1pm, 4pm and 7pm. Each booking lasts 3 hours; the room can be booked again when the slot ends.

P4 Private rooms only

Only the private rooms are booked: 6 rooms, 2 each for 6, 8 and 10 guests.

P5 Room by party size

1 to 6 guests: a 6-pax room. 7 to 8 guests: an 8-pax room. 9 to 10 guests: a 10-pax room. If that size is full, the next size up is offered, never a smaller one.

P6 Parties over 10

Parties of more than 10 are handed to the restaurant manager, who advises from what is free at the time.

P7 Walk-in tables

The hall tables are walk-in only and never booked: 15 tables of 4 in Daisy Court, 10 tables of 8 in Lily Court, 10 tables of 10 in Jasmine Court.

P8 Booking cut-off

A booking can be made up to 2 hours before the slot starts. A request for a slot that starts sooner goes to the restaurant manager.

P9 What a booking records

A booking records the date, the slot, the party size, the name and the phone number. There is no menu in the booking: guests order when they arrive.

P10 One booking per phone per slot

One booking per phone number per slot, on any route. The same request sent again gets the existing reference; nothing new is created. The same phone and slot with different details goes to the restaurant manager.

P11 Hold and confirmation

Every route ends in a room held for 15 minutes. A 6-digit code goes by SMS to the customer's phone. Only the customer's click on the confirmation screen, with the right code within the 15 minutes, confirms the booking. With no click the booking is not processed and the room is released. A change after the code is sent goes to the restaurant manager. Every confirmation, successful or not, is sent to the restaurant manager.

P12 Booking fee

Every confirmed booking carries a RM 100 booking fee, added to the bill at check-out with the meal. Nothing is collected at booking.

P13 Late arrival

The room is held for 15 minutes after the slot starts, then released.

P14 After booking

Changes, cancellations, overstay and no-show are handled by the restaurant manager and are outside this booking system.
