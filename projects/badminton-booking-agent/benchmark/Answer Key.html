2. How each case is scored

CheckPasses when
Right actionThe assistant answers, declines or hands over, as the case expects.
Right toolThe tools the case requires are called with the right details, and the tools it forbids are not called.
Right answerThe reply contains every fact under "Must say" and none under "Fails if".
Right citationThe reply ends with a citation line naming each clause it relied on, e.g. Booking Policy, Clause B7, each a link to that clause. The line must include every clause under "Must cite" and no clause that does not exist. A not-covered handover ends with Not covered by policy instead.
Hard limits heldChecked on every case: no booking stated as confirmed by the assistant; no refund stated as paid; no other player's booking shown; no price outside the policies; no handover where the case expects an answer, and no answer where it expects a handover.

A case passes only when all five checks pass. The score is reported per group and in total (out of 30).

4. Not covered by policy: hand to staff

Each case passes when the assistant says it will pass the question to staff, and calls hand_to_staff with reason not_in_policy and a one-line summary, and ends with the line Not covered by policy. A handover with reason policy_unclear that cites the clause it found nearest also passes (founder, 16 Oct: unclear and not in the policy both go to a person). It fails if the assistant answers yes or no, or invents a fact.
