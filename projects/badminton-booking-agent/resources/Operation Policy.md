# Operation Policy

Subang Badminton Arena (fictional demo centre). Cite clauses by ID, e.g. O9.

## 1. The centre

- **O1** Subang Badminton Arena, Subang Jaya, Selangor. 12 indoor courts, Court 1 to Court 12. *(Enforced by: Code (court list))*
- **O2** Every court has a rubber mat surface, LED lighting, air-conditioning and a scoreboard. All courts are the same and charged the same fee. *(Enforced by: Prompt)*
- **O3** Open every day, including public holidays, from 07:00 to 01:00. The last session ends at 01:00. *(Enforced by: Code (slot list))*
- **O4** The counter is staffed during opening hours. Payments are verified and requests reviewed only while the counter is open. *(Enforced by: Prompt)*

## 2. Prices

- **O5** The court fee, the deposit and when each is paid are set in the Booking Policy (B7 to B10). *(Enforced by: Code (fee table))*
- **O6** Fees are fixed. No discount, package, membership price or price match is offered through the assistant. *(Enforced by: Code (no discount tool))*
- **O7** The assistant quotes only the court fee, the deposit and the racket rental (O9). Any other price (coaching, events, shuttlecocks, merchandise) is quoted by staff. *(Enforced by: Prompt)*

## 3. Facilities

- **O8** Changing rooms with showers, drinking water and free parking for players. *(Enforced by: Prompt)*
- **O9** Racket rental at the counter: RM 5 per racket per booking, subject to stock. Rackets cannot be reserved in advance. *(Enforced by: Prompt)*
- **O10** Shuttlecocks are sold at the counter. Prices are given by staff. *(Enforced by: Prompt)*

## 4. House rules

- **O11** Non-marking indoor shoes only on court. *(Enforced by: Prompt)*
- **O12** Up to 6 players per court per session. *(Enforced by: Prompt)*
- **O13** No food on court. Bottled drinks are allowed. *(Enforced by: Prompt)*
- **O14** Check in at the counter at least 10 minutes before the session. Sessions end on time, whatever time play starts. *(Enforced by: Prompt)*

## 5. Payment

- **O15** Paid at booking (the deposit, and the court fee if the player pays it then): DuitNow QR only, with the booking reference in the payment reference field. *(Enforced by: Code (QR and reference shown by the tool))*
- **O16** Paid after the session (the court fee, if not paid at booking): at the counter, by cash, card or DuitNow QR. *(Enforced by: Prompt)*
- **O17** The assistant never receives, checks or confirms a payment. Staff check every payment against the centre's bank record. A receipt screenshot helps staff find the payment but does not confirm it. *(Enforced by: Code (no payment tool))*

## 6. Privacy

- **O18** Availability is shown as free or taken only. The assistant never names who booked a court. *(Enforced by: Code (availability tool returns no names))*
- **O19** A player sees only their own booking, after verification (Booking Policy B17). *(Enforced by: Code)*
- **O20** The assistant never lists bookings. *(Enforced by: Code (no list tool))*

## 7. Language

- **O21** The assistant replies in English. *(Enforced by: Prompt)*

## 8. How the assistant uses this policy

- Covered by policy: answer from this policy and cite the clause.
- Contradicts policy: decline, state the rule, offer what the rule does allow. No exceptions in chat.
- Not covered by policy: do not guess. Hand to staff (reason not_in_policy).
- Policy neither says yes nor no: do not choose a reading. Hand to staff (reason policy_unclear), naming the clause that seemed to apply.
