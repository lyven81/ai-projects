Cancellation Policy

1. Who can cancel

RulePolicyEnforced by
C1Only the player who made the booking, verified by booking reference and the last 4 digits of the mobile number (Booking Policy B17).Code
C2A held booking whose payment is not reported within 30 minutes is released by staff (Booking Policy B13). Nothing is charged.Code

2. Refund

RulePolicyEnforced by
C3A confirmed booking can be cancelled at any time before the session starts. The RM 50 deposit is refunded in full once the player is verified, with no notice period. A court fee paid at booking is refunded with it.Code (outcome calculated)
C4Refunds go to the account that paid, within 3 working days of staff approval.Prompt
C5Only a no-show forfeits the deposit: the player neither cancels before the session starts nor checks in. A court fee paid at booking is refunded.Code (outcome calculated)
C6Part of a booking cannot be cancelled. To play fewer hours, the player cancels the booking and books again.Code

3. How a cancellation is processed

RulePolicyEnforced by
C7The assistant tells the player the refund amount before submitting, and submits only when the player agrees.Code (outcome returned by the tool)
C8The request appears on the staff dashboard as a pending cancellation. The booking stays in place until staff approve.Code
C9On Approve, the court is released and the player receives an automatic message stating the deposit refunded (and the court fee, if paid at booking).Code (fixed message)
C10Refunds are paid by staff outside the chat. The assistant never refunds, waives or reduces an amount.Code (no refund tool)

4. Outside this policy

RulePolicyEnforced by
C11Requests to refund a no-show deposit are declined in chat with C5 stated. No exception is made by the assistant.Prompt
C12A session the centre cancels (power failure, court repair, an event) is handled by staff, who offer a full refund or another slot. The assistant hands these to staff.Prompt (handover reason)
C13Complaints, disputes about a refund, and refunds not received after 3 working days go to staff.Prompt (handover reason)

5. How the assistant uses this policy

EnquiryWhat the assistant does
Covered by policyAnswers from this policy and names the rule (e.g. C3).
Contradicts policyDeclines, states the rule that applies, and offers what the rule does allow. No exceptions are made in chat.
Not covered by policyDoes not guess. Hands the enquiry to staff with a short summary and the handover reason.
Policy neither says yes nor noDoes not choose a reading. Hands the enquiry to staff with the rule that seemed to apply and why it is unclear.

Rules marked Code are enforced by the booking tools, so the assistant cannot break them even if asked. Rules marked Prompt guide what the assistant says.
