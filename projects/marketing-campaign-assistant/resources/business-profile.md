# Comel Diaper: business profile (BP)

- **BP-1 Business.** Comel Diaper is a baby diaper retailer selling diapers, pants and wipes online and through WhatsApp orders. The owner runs the business alone and runs the advertising personally.
- **BP-2 Platforms.** Facebook, Instagram, TikTok, YouTube and XiaoHongShu. Currency RM.
- **BP-3 Review period.** Monday 29 September 2025 to Sunday 27 September 2026 (52 weeks). Review cutoff: Sunday 27 September 2026.
- **BP-4 Action window.** The owner makes approved changes on the platforms and records them between Monday 28 September and Friday 2 October 2026.
- **BP-5 Next period.** Monday 28 September to Sunday 25 October 2026 (4 weeks). Its results can be read only after it closes, from Monday 26 October 2026.
- **BP-6 Data.** Sample data: 60 Sales campaigns and their weekly results. Platform-reported figures. Data issues the owner has reported are listed in `data/data_notes.json`.
- **BP-7 The owner decides.** The assistant reviews, explains and proposes. The owner approves or rejects each proposal, makes the change on the platform and records it.
- **BP-8 Owner settings** (held in `config.json`). Minimum ROAS: not set. Maximum cost per order: not set. The owner may enter both on the workspace (kept in that browser); they then apply to that workspace and its chat. Proposed figures are shown beside the boxes: the median ROAS and the median cost per order of the Completed Sales campaigns over the review period, comparison benchmarks the owner may accept or replace; they never become targets unless the owner enters them. Test caps (demo limits): RM 500 per test over its four weeks, RM 1,000 for all tests together.
