# Planning rules (PR)

- **PR-1 Change types.** Routine: pause, budget_shift (a new weekly budget), extend (a later end date), end (an earlier end date). Tests (PR-12): test_boost (extra weekly budget on a Running campaign) and test_new (a new campaign on a platform). Only Running campaigns can be changed.
- **PR-2 Always pending.** Every proposal is saved as pending. Only the owner approves or rejects, on the workspace.
- **PR-3 Supported proposal.** The reason cites figure IDs returned earlier in the same conversation. For a routine change, the cited figures are for the same campaign, cover at least 3 complete weeks, and include no missing week. A test cites the campaign's or its platform's figures, without the 3-week history.
- **PR-4 Campaign cap.** A campaign's weekly budget may move by at most plus or minus 20% of its current weekly budget in the next period, counting pending and approved shifts and boosts together. A test boost stays inside this cap.
- **PR-5 Total budget.** The planned next-period budget of all Running campaigns plus every new campaign test (weekly budgets x 4, after pending and approved changes; a paused or ended campaign counts 0) may not exceed this period's spend: the Running campaigns' spend Monday 31 August to Sunday 27 September 2026.
- **PR-6 Target.** Every proposal carries one target from T1 to T4 with: baseline (a figure ID over Monday 31 August to Sunday 27 September 2026, of the target's measure: the campaign's, or for a new campaign test the platform's, labelled a comparison benchmark), deadline (a Sunday between 11 and 25 October 2026), and the measure with its threshold.
  | Rule | For | Measure and threshold |
  |---|---|---|
  | T1 Stop | pause, end | Weekly spend RM 0 in every full week counted |
  | T2 Scale or test | budget_shift up (PR-11), test_boost, test_new | Main measure holds within 10% of baseline (ROAS at least 0.9 x baseline; a cost measure at most 1.1 x baseline) |
  | T3 Trim | budget_shift down | Main measure at or better than baseline (ROAS at least baseline; a cost measure at most baseline) |
  | T4 Extend | extend | Main measure within 10% of baseline, as T2 |
  Main measure: ROAS. T1 to T4 measure the follow-up only: meeting one never shows profitability or that the change caused it.
- **PR-7 Check window.** The check counts the full Monday to Sunday weeks starting after the recorded action date, up to the deadline. The week the action fell in is never counted.
- **PR-8 Status** (what the owner recorded, shown as Status; field `done`): reported done (the owner's action record exists), not reported (approved, no record), conflict (a record of pause or end, but spend continues in a counted week). Owner records are reported execution, never verified.
- **PR-9 Target met?** met, missed, or not yet assessable (no counted week, or the measure is undefined because results are zero). Kept separate from Status. A met target never proves the change caused it. A new campaign test reads "test results unavailable": the sample data holds no rows for a campaign that did not exist, and no results are generated in response to an approval. A boost is checked on the campaign's existing next-period rows, which were written in advance and do not respond to the change.
- **PR-10 Next period hidden.** Next-period results are read only by the check, and only after the period closes.
- **PR-11 Owner targets and routine changes.** Without both owner targets (BP-8): no routine budget increase; every increase goes through a test (PR-12). With both targets set, a routine increase (budget_shift up, within PR-4 and PR-5, target T2) is allowed only when the campaign's review status over 31 Aug to 27 Sep 2026 is Consistent results and it meets both targets (RP-11). Meets one: maintain and investigate, no increase. Meets neither: eligible for a reduction. Either way, trims, pauses and earlier ends need a documented performance reason (PR-3) and owner approval, and extensions need a T4 target. Eligibility is permission to propose, not proof that scaling will work.
- **PR-12 Tests.** A test needs a business reason with cited figures, a test question, a stop condition and a T2 target (the success measure and the review date); its duration is the four-week next period. Caps: RM 500 per test over its four weeks (for test_boost the additional spending, not the existing budget; for test_new its whole budget) and RM 1,000 for all pending and approved tests together, both inside PR-5; a boost also stays inside PR-4. A test is checked when it is requested: the caps, the proposal fields and data issues. A campaign, or for a new test its platform, whose review status over 31 Aug to 27 Sep 2026 is Data issue cannot take a test until the issue is investigated. A review status alone never makes a campaign eligible.
