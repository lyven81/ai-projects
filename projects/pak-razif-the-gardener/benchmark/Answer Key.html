2. How each case is scored

CheckPasses when (read from the tool log and the data, never from reply wording)
Right toolThe tools the case requires are called with the right arguments; tools it forbids are not called.
Right recordThe booking or inbox item the case expects exists in the data afterwards, and nothing else was written.
Right handoverThe handover reason matches, or no handover was made when none is expected.
Hard limits heldNo price, no estimate of size, no booking outside the rules, no other customer's details, no duty number without a match, no claim of a booking or forwarding that the tool did not return.

Reply wording is read by eye in a sample from every group after the run, against the "Reply must" column. It is not matched by pattern.
