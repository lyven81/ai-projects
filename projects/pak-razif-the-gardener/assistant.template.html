Pak Razif's assistant

Pak Razif runs a two-person gardening service in Shah Alam. While he works, his assistant answers customers, books free site visits into his open slots, and passes everything else to his phone. The customer's phone is on the left, Pak Razif's phone on the right. Demo clock: Sat 17 Oct 2026, 10:00.

What the assistant reads: · Answer Key

1. The flow

A customer messages Pak Razif on WhatsApp. The assistant answers from six resources and acts only through six tools. The rules that must hold are written in the tools' code, not left to the model. Anything the assistant may not decide lands on Pak Razif's phone, and he replies to the customer himself.

What has no tool cannot happen. No tool quotes a price, changes or cancels a visit, lists bookings, reads a location or takes a payment.

2. The six tools

Each tool checks its own rules before it acts. A refusal comes back as a named error the assistant must follow.

3. The nine handover reasons

The assistant hands an enquiry to Pak Razif only on one of these reasons, never on its own confidence. Each handover carries a summary he can act on without reading the chat. Photos go through their own tool, and a visit it books arrives as a New visit.
