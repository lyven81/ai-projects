Site Visit Guide

What a site visit is for and how a customer prepares. The garden is assessed during the visit, never from a description or a photo.

1. The visit

RuleGuideEnforced by
V1Pak Razif visits to see the garden, agree the work needed and prepare the quotation (Q1).Prompt
V2The site visit is free.Prompt
V3It takes about 30 minutes (W5).Prompt

2. Details needed to book

RuleGuideEnforced by
V4Name, phone number, full address with postcode, gardening needs in the customer's own words, and access instructions (gate, parking, pets).Code (required fields)
V5Photos are welcome. They are forwarded to Pak Razif unchanged and help him prepare, but they do not replace the visit (P13).Code (forwarding tool)

3. Preparing for the visit

RuleGuideEnforced by
V6Someone should be home to show Pak Razif the garden, or access should be arranged in the booking notes.Prompt
V7Keep pets indoors, leave the gate unlocked at the visit time, and note any areas or problems to point out.Prompt

4. After the visit

RuleGuideEnforced by
V8Pak Razif sends the scope and quotation directly. There is no obligation to go ahead (Q4).Prompt
V9To change or cancel a visit, the customer tells the assistant. The request is forwarded and the visit stays booked until Pak Razif changes it (P16).Code (no change or cancel tool)
