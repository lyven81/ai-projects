# FAQ

Pak Razif Garden (fictional demo business). Cite clauses by ID, e.g. F2.

Short answers to the questions customers ask most. Each answer comes from the rules it cites.

## 1. Services and quotations

- **F1** Q: What gardening services do you offer? A: Grass cutting and edge trimming, hedge and bush trimming, garden clean-up and garden waste removal. Pak Razif confirms the work for your garden after a site visit. *(From: P5)*
- **F2** Q: How much will it cost? A: Pak Razif confirms the scope and quotation after a site visit. I can help book the visit. *(From: R1, Q1)*
- **F3** Q: Can you estimate the price from my photos? A: No. Photos are passed to Pak Razif unchanged, and he assesses the garden during the visit. *(From: Q2, P13)*
- **F4** Q: Is the site visit free? A: Yes, the site visit is free and takes about 30 minutes. *(From: V2, V3)*
- **F5** Q: Can you do something not on the list? A: I will pass your request to Pak Razif for assessment. *(From: P6)*

## 2. Area

- **F6** Q: Do you cover my area? A: Shah Alam, Klang and Subang Jaya. Share your postcode and I will check it. *(From: A1, A2, A3)*
- **F7** Q: My area is nearby but not listed. A: That location needs Pak Razif’s approval. Please share the address and work required for his review. *(From: A4, R5)*
- **F8** Q: Do you take jobs outside Selangor? A: Outstation jobs are passed to Pak Razif for assessment. *(From: A5)*

## 3. Booking a site visit

- **F9** Q: When can Pak Razif visit? A: Monday to Saturday, at 08:00, 10:00, 14:00 or 16:00. I can check the open slots for your preferred day. *(From: W1, W3)*
- **F10** Q: How do I book? A: Tell me your preferred day, then your name, phone, address with postcode, what you need done and how to get in. I reserve the slot and confirm it straight away. *(From: P8, P9, V4)*
- **F11** Q: The time I want is not available. A: That slot is unavailable. I can offer another available time or pass your request to Pak Razif. *(From: R4, W12)*
- **F12** Q: Can I send photos? A: Yes. They are passed to Pak Razif unchanged to help him prepare. *(From: P13, V5)*
- **F13** Q: What should I prepare? A: Have someone home or arrange access, keep pets indoors, and note any problem areas to show him. *(From: V6, V7)*

## 4. After booking

- **F14** Q: How far ahead can I book? A: From tomorrow up to the same date next month. For a visit today or further ahead, I pass your request to Pak Razif. *(From: W8, W9)*
- **F15** Q: Can I change or cancel my visit? A: I can pass your request to Pak Razif, and he will get back to you. Your appointment remains booked until he changes it. *(From: P16, R7, R10)*
- **F16** Q: Is Pak Razif on his way? A: I can’t check his location or arrival time. Share your booking reference and the last 4 digits of your phone number, and I’ll give you his number. *(From: R6, P15, P18)*
- **F17** Q: When can the work start after the visit? A: Pak Razif agrees the work date with you directly after the quotation. *(From: Q5, P29)*
- **F18** Q: I have a complaint or something was damaged. A: I’m sorry to hear this. Please describe what happened so I can pass your concern to Pak Razif. *(From: R8, P35)*
- **F19** Q: How do I pay? A: Payment terms come with Pak Razif’s quotation. Payment questions are passed to him. *(From: Q6, P21)*
