Work Schedule

The site visit slots the assistant may book. Which slots are open on a given day comes from Pak Razif's calendar, read live through the availability tool, never from this page.

1. Visit days

RuleScheduleEnforced by
W1Monday to Saturday. Sunday is a rest day.Code (slot list)
W2Closed on Selangor public holidays. The dates are blocked in the calendar.Code (calendar)

2. Site visit slots

RuleScheduleEnforced by
W3Visit slots: 08:00 and 10:00 in the morning, 14:00 and 16:00 in the afternoon.Code (slot list)
W4Midday rest: no visits or field work from 12:00 to 14:00, outside the hottest hours.Code (slot list)
W5Each visit takes about 30 minutes, with a 30-minute travel buffer before the next slot.Code (slot list)
W6The time window given to the customer is the slot start plus 30 minutes, for example 08:00 to 08:30.Code (confirmation text)
W7At most 4 visits a day.Code (slot list)
W8Booking window: from the day after the customer's first contact up to the same date in the following month (the last day of that month if it is shorter). First contact on 30 Oct allows bookings up to 30 Nov. The date of first contact stays fixed if the customer returns later. A request beyond the window is forwarded to Pak Razif without booking (P10).Code (booking tool)
W9Same-day requests: a visit on the day of the enquiry is forwarded to Pak Razif, even if a slot later that day is open. The assistant does not reserve the slot (R11).Code (booking tool)

3. Availability and booking

RuleScheduleEnforced by
W10Open slots come from Pak Razif's calendar. He blocks any slot he cannot keep and can open any blocked slot (P38).Code (availability tool)
W11The slot is reserved before the visit is confirmed. Two customers cannot take the same slot: the booking tool checks again when it reserves.Code (booking tool)
W12If no open slot suits the customer, the assistant offers open slots on nearby days, or forwards the request without booking (P27).Code (handover reason)
W13Pak Razif's field work (gardening jobs agreed after quotations) sits in the same calendar. A visit slot that clashes with field work is blocked, so it is not offered unless Pak Razif opens it.Code (calendar)
