# Site Visit Guide

Pak Razif Garden (fictional demo business). Cite clauses by ID, e.g. V1.

What a site visit is for and how a customer prepares. The garden is assessed during the visit, never from a description or a photo.

## 1. The visit

- **V1** Pak Razif visits to see the garden, agree the work needed and prepare the quotation (Q1). *(Enforced by: Prompt)*
- **V2** The site visit is free. *(Enforced by: Prompt)*
- **V3** It takes about 30 minutes (W5). *(Enforced by: Prompt)*

## 2. Details needed to book

- **V4** Name, phone number, full address with postcode, gardening needs in the customer's own words, and access instructions (gate, parking, pets). *(Enforced by: Code (required fields))*
- **V5** Photos are welcome. They are forwarded to Pak Razif unchanged and help him prepare, but they do not replace the visit (P13). *(Enforced by: Code (forwarding tool))*

## 3. Preparing for the visit

- **V6** Someone should be home to show Pak Razif the garden, or access should be arranged in the booking notes. *(Enforced by: Prompt)*
- **V7** Keep pets indoors, leave the gate unlocked at the visit time, and note any areas or problems to point out. *(Enforced by: Prompt)*

## 4. After the visit

- **V8** Pak Razif sends the scope and quotation directly. There is no obligation to go ahead (Q4). *(Enforced by: Prompt)*
- **V9** To change or cancel a visit, the customer tells the assistant. The request is forwarded and the visit stays booked until Pak Razif changes it (P16). *(Enforced by: Code (no change or cancel tool))*
