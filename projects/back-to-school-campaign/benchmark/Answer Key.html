AI Projects / Kiddoz Back-to-School Campaign Team

Answer Key

Purpose: the 16 paid benchmark scenarios for the Kiddoz campaign team, each with the request, the expected tools and what must hold. Approved by the founder on Wednesday 28 October 2026, before the build.

Setting: Kiddoz Uniform House, a school uniform niche shop in Bangi, Selangor. Owner Puan Zarina Ahmad sends every request. The campaign runs from Monday 26 October 2026 to Friday 30 October 2026.

Test calendar: Wednesday 28 October 2026, 10:00 Malaysia time. Each scenario starts from fresh state, except the linked steps in group D.

Source: the price list resource kiddoz://price-list (the 100 campaign SKUs, about 20% of the shop's stock), the store profile and the channel guide. If a resource changes, this key changes with it.

SKU citing: every fact, claim and finding names a product by its SKU, never by name alone. Fifteen product names repeat across primary and secondary at different prices (PB01 RM20.00 on promotion against SB01 RM24.00).

How it is scored: from the tool log, the structured facts, the writer outputs, the hook results, the reviewer findings and the delivery record. Exact wording is not prescribed; copy quality has its own human score (section 11).

2. How each scenario is scored

CheckPasses when
Right routeThe run delivers, asks the owner with the expected reason, or stops, as the scenario expects.
Right toolsThe tools the scenario expects are called in the expected order, and no other writer starts.
Right factsEvery SKU, price, discount and date in the copy and the claims list matches the price list.
Right statusEach requested channel ends with the expected status name.
Hard limits heldAll ten hard limits in section 3 hold.

A scenario passes only when all five checks pass. A linked scenario counts as one run, with every step scored.

3. Hard limits

#Hard limit, checked on every run
1extract_facts runs first.
2Every SKU, price, discount and date in the copy matches the price list; discounts are recalculated and are at most 20% per item.
3Nothing missing or contradictory is filled in silently.
4No banned claim: cheapest, guarantee, gift, voucher, free delivery, stock or scarcity, search ranking, whole shop on sale.
5Writers are started dynamically, at most five, all in one coordinator response.
6Every requested channel is accounted for before review and at delivery.
7Each writer uses only its own tool. The reviewer gets the facts, the owner's requirements and the full copy, never the writers' reasoning.
8Only failing channels are redone, and the next review uses the current copy.
9Caps are enforced by the runner; no agent approves or posts.
10Every tool-log line carries the agent card.

The PostToolUse hook checks the facts written in the copy itself: every RM figure, percentage and date, and any stock, scarcity or banned phrase. It checks the copy text, not only the writer's separate claims list.

11. Copy quality

The founder scores one delivered output per channel on four dimensions. A pass is 4 of 5 on every dimension. A factual error fails a hard limit regardless of the writing.

DimensionWhat 4 of 5 means
Natural Bahasa MelayuClear and natural, with only minor edits needed
Channel suitabilityReads as the requested WhatsApp message, Instagram caption, email sequence, blog or search copy
Persuasive usefulnessGives parents a clear reason to consider the offer and a next action
Tone and campaign consistencyMatches the chosen tone and presents the same offer across channels

12. Runs, timing and cost

ItemPlan
RunsThe 15 other scenarios once each, and A1 six times: three in parallel and three run sequentially. About 21 runs.
Sequential A1Same brief, prompts and models. The runner executes the five writer calls one after another.
MeasuresTotal time, writing-phase time, overlap, retries, model calls, tokens, cost
Writing-phase speedupSequential median writing time divided by parallel median writing time
Total time reduction(Sequential median total minus parallel median total) divided by sequential median total, times 100
Cost per completed campaignActual API cost, review and retries included
Completion rateRequested channels delivered ready_for_approval divided by requested channels
First-attempt pass rateWriter outputs passing the hook and review with no correction, divided by first writer outputs
ThresholdsNo speed or cost threshold is set before the sample gives a baseline. A cost estimate is written after the 3-case sample.

13. Acceptance

  • The owner-question routes and the two failure cases behave as expected.
  • Every hard limit holds.
  • Valid requests end with the requested channels ready_for_approval, including after a correction or a clarification.
  • Unrecoverable scenarios end clearly waiting_for_owner or failed.
  • Delivered copy meets the quality bar in section 11.
  • Timing and cost are reported with actual measurements, retries and unsuccessful runs included.
