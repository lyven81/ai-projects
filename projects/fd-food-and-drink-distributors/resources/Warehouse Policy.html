AI Projects / FD Food and Drink Distributors

Warehouse Policy

FD Food and Drink Distributors is a fictional Selangor wholesaler of packaged food, drinks, sauces and cooking ingredients, used for demonstration. This policy is an MCP resource: the staff assistant reads it, and the hooks around its five tools cite these clauses when they refuse.

Enforced by: PreToolUse the hook checks before the tool runs; PostToolUse the hook checks or converts the result; Back end the data layer repeats the check on its own; Policy no tool exists, so the assistant answers from the clause and names who decides.

The other policies: Management Policy, Sales Policy, Finance Policy.

1. Stock

ClauseRuleEnforced by
WP-01Two warehouses, WH-A Shah Alam and WH-B Puchong. Each customer is served from the warehouse for its location, as set in the location table (WP-08).Back end
WP-02Stock is checked per warehouse. Sales and Warehouse may check stock; Finance may not (MP-07).PreToolUse, Back end
WP-0324 hours is the maximum age of a stock figure. A figure within 24 hours is recent enough to quote, not proof that the stock is still there. A figure older than 24 hours is out of date: no order is created on it until the warehouse manager confirms it by a manual count.PostToolUse, PreToolUse
WP-04Stock is rechecked and reserved by the back end when the order is created, together with the credit.Back end
WP-05When stock is short, the salesperson decides whether to order the quantity available, with a new confirmation of the summary. There are no back orders. The warehouse delivers what Sales has ordered.PreToolUse
WP-06Transfers between the two warehouses are decided by the warehouse manager only, outside the assistant.Policy

2. Delivery

ClauseRuleEnforced by
WP-07Delivery terms, in Malaysia time (UTC+8): an order confirmed before 3 pm on a working day is delivered the next working day; an order confirmed at or after 3 pm, or on a non-working day, is delivered on the second working day. Working days are Monday to Saturday, except Selangor public holidays listed in WP-09. An earlier or special delivery date needs the warehouse manager's recorded approval.PreToolUse
WP-08The location table below: each customer location and the warehouse that serves it.Back end
WP-09The public holiday table below: the Selangor public holidays for the period the demo covers, September to December 2026.PreToolUse
WP-10Warehouse staff check stock and schedule deliveries within the terms. They do not create orders or see credit.PreToolUse, Back end
WP-11An order's delivery address cannot be changed after 3 pm Malaysia time on the working day before delivery, when routes are planned. Before that, an address change is made by the sales manager on the customer record (MP-13). After it, the order is delivered as planned, or cancelled and placed again (SP-10). No tool changes an order's address; the assistant answers such a request from this clause.Policy
