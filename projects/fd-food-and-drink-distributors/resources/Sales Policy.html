Sales Policy

FD Food and Drink Distributors is a fictional Selangor wholesaler of packaged food, drinks, sauces and cooking ingredients, used for demonstration. This policy is an MCP resource: the staff assistant reads it, and the hooks around its five tools cite these clauses when they refuse.

Enforced by: PreToolUse the hook checks before the tool runs; PostToolUse the hook checks or converts the result; Back end the data layer repeats the check on its own; Policy no tool exists, so the assistant answers from the clause and names who decides.

The other policies: Management Policy, Finance Policy, Warehouse Policy.

1. Orders

ClauseRuleEnforced by
SP-01Orders are created by Sales Tier 02 only, for their own assigned customers (MP-06). The sales manager does not create orders; the sales manager approves the team's requests and may read any order summary.PreToolUse, Back end
SP-02An order lists catalogue SKUs in their ordering unit, in whole units.PreToolUse
SP-03Minimum order value RM 300. A smaller order needs the sales manager's recorded approval.PreToolUse
SP-04Prices are the customer's own price list, held in the back end and served only by retrieve_customer_prices. An SKU with no price on that list cannot be ordered until the sales manager sets one.PostToolUse, PreToolUse
SP-05Promotions apply only between their start and end dates, as listed in the promotion calendar (SP-12). A promotion is a published price and applies without approval.PostToolUse, PreToolUse
SP-06Every discount needs the sales manager's recorded approval, up to 15%; nothing above 15%. Sales Tier 02 staff cannot give a discount on their own.PreToolUse
SP-07The staff member confirms the full order summary on the staff page before the order is created: customer, SKUs and quantities, prices, discount, total, payment route and delivery date. A change to any of these needs a new confirmation.PreToolUse
SP-08Every order request carries a unique request ID. A retry with the same request ID returns the order already created and never creates a second one. The same applies to a credit raise.PreToolUse, Back end
SP-09Separately, the same order for the same customer (same SKUs and quantities) under a new request ID within 30 minutes is treated as a likely duplicate and not created; the staff member is asked.PreToolUse
SP-10A confirmed order may be cancelled before dispatch by the sales manager only, outside the assistant. After dispatch it cannot be cancelled.Policy
SP-11Order confirmations to customers are sent outside the assistant, only to an approved contact stored against the customer, with text taken from the saved order. The assistant has no tool for them.Policy
SP-12The promotion calendar is the table below: promotion, SKUs, discount, start and end date. Its promotion prices are published prices, not discounts under SP-06.PostToolUse
